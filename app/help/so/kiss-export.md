Qaybta {{ui:Wax soo saar}}, fur {{ui:Dheeraad}} oo dooro {{ui:Faylka KISS ee software-ka soo saarka}}. DetailCraft wuxuu soo dejiyaa `<name>.kss`, fayl KISS ("Keep It Simple, Steel", nooca 1.1), qaab qoraal oo fudud oo barnaamijyada maaraynta soo saarka badankood ka soo dejiyaan liiska alaabta.

## Waxa ku jira

- Madaxa: lambarka shaqada (sideed xaraf iyo lambar oo ka mid ah magaca moodeelka), magaca moodeelka, taariikhda iyo waqtiga, iyo haddii cabbirradu yihiin metric.
- Isku-dhis kasta oo calaamad leh: qaybtiisa ugu weyn (tirada = inta isku-dhis ee la jiro), safka naqshadda (A-B1 iyo sidiisa, lambarka naqshadda isku-dhiska), safka isku-dhiska (magaca, nooca, culeyska, dherer guud, qoto iyo ballac), safka taxanaha ee [marxalad](topic:phases) kasta oo isku-dhiska ku jirto, iyo safka shuqulka: godadka (intee le'eg, dhexroorka, dhumucda oo dhan), jarka iyo alxanka.
- Dabadeed qayb kasta oo isku-dhiskaas ku xidhan, oo leh tirada **wadarta** (midkiiba × isku-dhisyada), sida heerku sheegayo.
- Boolasha dhex-socda iyo ulaha aasaaska naqshadda BOLTS, isku-dhiska FIELD.
- Safka kasta waxa uu sheegaa nooca (W, C, L, HSS, PL, ama MI oo ah profile gaar ah), cabbirka, darajada, dherer jarka oo millimitir ah, magaca qaybta iyo culeyska.

## Imperial ama metric

Dejinta [DetailCraft](topic:settings), **Cabbirrada faylka KISS** waxay doorataa:

- **Toos** (bilowga): imperial markii xubin kasta ay tahay qaab AISC, haddii kale metric.
- **Imperial**: qaabab sida calaamadooda AISC (W 16X26, HSS 4X4X.250); saxannada iyo boolasha, oo DetailCraft u hayo millimitir, ilaa 1/16 in oo ugu dhow; culeysyada pound.
- **Metric**: qaabab Yurub sida magacooda (IPE300), saxannada sida dhumucda X ballaca millimitir; culeysyada kilogram.

Dherer waa mar walba millimitir oo leh laba meel oo toban, si kasta oo loo dejiyo: heerka KISS ayaa waxaas codsada, barnaamijka la siinayaana wuu beddelaa.

:::note
Darajooyinka waxa lagu gaabiyaa siddeed xaraf oo heerka ah: A572 Gr 50 wuxuu noqdaa A572-50. Calaamadaha magacyada ku jira waxay noqdaan meel bannaan (qaabku ma leeyahay xidhid). Hal import ka hubi barnaamijkaaga ka hor intaadan isku hallayn: faylalka KISS ee DetailCraft waxaa lagu hubiyaa heerka halkan, weli ma jiraan barnaamij soo saarka lagu hubiyo.
:::
