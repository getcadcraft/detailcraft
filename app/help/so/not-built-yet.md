DetailCraft waa dhis hore. Bogaan wuxuu ku liistaa waxa weli aan samayn, si aad qorshe ugu samayso. Waxba halkan kuma jiro ballan taariikh.

## Moodeelka

| Weli ma jiro | Halkii, maanta |
|---|---|
| Jiidista hal dhamaad xubin (dhetharka) | Wax ka beddel {{ui:Bilow}} ama {{ui:Dhamaad}} hoosta {{ui:Dhamaadyada (x, y, z mm)}} panelka {{ui:Sifooyinka}} |
| Shaandhe doorasho (kaliya biimaha, kaliya hal profile) | Jiid daaqad, kadibna {{key:Shift}}+click si aad qaybaha uga saarto |
| Array doorasho; wareeg dhinaca janjeersan; muraayad ku samee dhinaca janjeersan | Koobiyee oo dhaji meel kale, ama {{ui:Laba-jibbaari}}; wareeg iyo muraayad waxay ku shaqeeyaan qorshaha ([Dooro daaqad, guuri, wareeg, muraayad, beddel badan](topic:select-and-change)) |
| Barta ka baxsan gridka markii laga riixayo | Ku dhig gridka, kadibna ku qor isku xidhka dhamaadka |
| Muuqaalka dhinaca ee khadadka gridka lambarada leh; muuqaalo la kaydiyay; qorshe-shaqo | Isticmaal {{ui:3D}}, ama {{ui:Ku dar gridka}} |
| Godadka oo lagu muujiyo gooyo dhab ah muuqaalka 3D | Godadku waxay u muuqdaan sinaajiyo mugdi ah; waa dhab ah dhoofinta oo dhan |

| Wax ka beddelka ama ka saarista profile gaar ah | Ku dar profile la saxay oo magac cusub ku bixi |

## Xidhiidhada

DetailCraft wuxuu dhisaa lix xidhiidh: gusset xidhe (eeg [Brace gussets](topic:brace-gussets)); xidhid boolt ah oo u dhexeeya laba qaybood oo I-shape ah oo isku qayb ah (eeg [Splices](topic:splices)); mitre u dhexeeya laba I-shape oo kulmaya xagal, gooyo kaliya ama saxan dhamaad boolt ah (eeg [Mitre joints](topic:mitres)); saxan dhamaad boolt ah oo fidsan ama dheereeyay, laga bilaabo biim I-shape oo toosan, kor u taagan, ilaa flange ama web tiir I-shape ah; shear tab laga bilaabo biim (labajibbaaran, janjeersan ama hoos u dhacaysa) ilaa flange ama web tiir; iyo saxan saldhig tiir oo leh afar anchor rod.

| Weli ma jiro | Halkii, maanta |
|---|---|
| Saxanada dhamaadka janjeersan ama hoos u dhacaysa | Isticmaal [shear tabs](topic:shear-tabs), oo qaata biimaha janjeersan iyo kuwa hoos u dhacaya |
| Xagallada clip; saxanada buuxinta splice-ka iyo splice-yada cabbir kala duwan; gusset-yada geeska ee lagu alxaamay biimaha iyo tiirka; gusset-yada la jarey | Ku faahfaahi softwareka kale, ama ku dar saxano iyo godad gacanta sidii qaybo furan; eeg [Splices](topic:splices), [Brace gussets](topic:brace-gussets) |
| Stiffeners-ka saldhigga ee la jarey ama dhumuc yar; godadka grout-ka; godka aasaaska ee shear lug-ka | Stiffeners afar-geesle; eeg [Base plates](topic:base-plates) |
| Haunches; mitre-yo alxaamay oo leh butt weld; stiffeners-ka oo toosan flange-yada xubinta kale | [Mitre](topic:mitres) oo leh saxanada dhamaadka iyo web stiffeners, ama mitre gooyo toosan; ku dar saxano kale gacanta sidii qaybo furan |
| Hubinta naqshadda xidhiidhka (xoogga) | Hubi xidhiidhada xisaabaadka injineerkaaga; DetailCraft wuxuu keliya digaa masaafada geesta iyo masaafada |
| Godadka dalool-ka | Godad wareega oo kaliya |

## Falanqeyn

| Weli ma jiro | Halkii, maanta |
|---|---|
| Hubinta naqshadda ka baxsan biimaha I ee birta: tuubooyin, xagallo, kanaalo, qaabab alxaamay, qaybo dhuuban ama class 4, torsion, xidhiidh; kontreetiga: dhuuqsanaanta sway-ga, torsion, faahfaahinta dhulgariirka, dabaqyada iyo derbiyada | Tab-ka {{ui:Falanqayn}} wuxuu hubiyaa biimaha birta I-shape ee AISC 360 ama EN 1993-1-1 ([Hubi xubnaha birta xeerka naqshadda](topic:design-checks)); kuwa kale wuxuu ku calaamadeeyaa "lama hubin"; injineer ayaa dhan dib u eegaa |
| Falanqaynta buckling-ka, gariirka | Second order waa P-Delta oo kaliya (elastic); ma jiro falanqaynta buckling-ka (modal, response spectrum, time history iyo pushover waxay ku jiraan tab-ka Falanqayn) |
| Hubinta naqshadda ee injineer dib u eegay | Waa caawin ilaa markaas: hubi laba ama saddex xubnood gacanta ama softwareka injineerkaaga ka hor intaadan ku tiirsanaan |
| Culeyska sagxadda, dabaysha iyo barafka oo laga sameeyo agaarka | Culeyska xubnaha gacanta ku dhig, kN/m ama kN |
| Kala-saarista xubnaha, xidhiidhada eccentric-ka ah, xidheyaasha xannaanta-kaliya, saxanada iyo aasaaska qaab-dhismeedka | Xubnaha waxaa lagu falanqeeyaa khadadka shaqadooda; xidheyaashu waxay qaadaan xannaan iyo cadaad |

## Madasha

- DetailCraft waxaa loo sameeyay shaashadda desktop-ka, laptop-ka ama tablet-ka. Telefoonka way shaqeysaa: moodeelku wuxuu haystaa shaashadda iyo panelada, warbixinnadana waxay ka furmaan xaashiyo bar-ka hoose, laakiin meel yar ayaa panelada u jirta.
- Isticmaalka khadka ka baxsan wuxuu u baahan yahay in DetailCraft marka hore loo kaydiyo khadka ka baxsan, adigoo isticmaalaya {{ui:Kaydi khadka ka baxsan}} bar-ka app-ka; markii uu akhriyo {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}}, wax kasta oo dhisme kan wuu shaqeeyaa iyada oo aan xiriir jirin. Eeg [Shaqaynta khadka ka baxsan](common:offline).

Haddii mid ka mid ah xaddidaadyadan uu xannibo shaqadaada, nala soo xidhiidh bogga [bogga taageerada](https://getcadcraft.com/support); waxay naga caawinaysaa inaan go'aano waxa soo socda ee aan dhisno.
