DetailCraft wuxuu leeyahay 2,311 cabbir oo dhismay, mid kasta oo laga nuqlay miis la daabacay, oo tijaabooyinka app-ka hubiyaan: area-ga laga xisaabiyo cabbirrada waa inuu la mid noqdaa area-ga la daabacay, miisaanka la daabacay waa inuu la mid noqdaa area-ga oo lagu dhufto dhuufnaanta birta. Saxanada iyo profile-yada gaarka ah waxaa laga sameeyaa cabbirradooda.

## Magacaabid

- **Qaybaha US** waxaa loo qoraa sida AISC Manual-ku u daabaco, xaraf yar x: W12x50, C10x15.3, L4x4x1/2, HSS6x6x1/4, HSS6.625x0.280. Qorista ma kala saarto xarfaha weyn iyo kuwa yar.
- **Pipe** waxaa loo qoraa sida AISC database-ka: Pipe4STD, Pipe4XS, Pipe4XXS (caadi, aad u adag, aad u adag oo laba jibbaar).
- **Qaybaha Yurub** waxaa loo qoraa sida EN 10365: IPE200, HEA240, HEB300, UPN200 (aan meel u dhexayn).
- **Qaybaha UK** waxaa loo qoraa familiga marka hore, kadibna cabbirka Blue Book-ka: UB305x165x40, UC203x203x46, PFC200x90x30, EA100x100x10.0, UEA150x90x10.0, SHS100x100x5.0, RHS200x100x8.0, CHS88.9x5.0. Tuubooyinka qaabka qabow ah waxay ku bilaabmaan CF: CFSHS100x100x5.0. Ku qorista sida ay badanaa naqshadaha ku yiraahdaan waa sidoo kale shaqeysaa: 305x165x40 UB, L100x100x10, SHS100x5.
- **Saxanada** waa `PL<dhumuc>x<ballac>` millimitir: PL20x200 waa 20 mm dhumuc iyo 200 mm ballac. Cabbir kasta oo ka timid qalabka {{ui:Saxan}} waa shaqeysaa.
- **Profile-yada gaarka ah** magac kasta oo aad siiso ayey lahaan karaan, ilaa iyo inaanu ahayn magac catalog-ka ama mid u eg magaca saxanka.

Liisaska {{ui:Muuqaal dherer}}, madax kasta oo koox ah wuxuu magacaabaa isha, tusaale "W shapes — AISC Shapes Database v16.0".

## Ilaha

| Koox | Isha | Faahfaahin |
|---|---|---|
| W shapes, S shapes, Channels (C), Angles (L), HSS, Pipe | AISC Shapes Database v16.0 | Cabbirrada laga beddelay inch-ka millimitir si sax ah; miisaanka mitir kasta laga helay lb/ft la daabacay |
| IPE, HEA, HEB, UPN | EN 10365:2017 | Millimitir iyo kg/m sida la daabacay |
| UB, UC, PFC, EA, UEA, SHS, RHS, CHS, CFSHS, CFRHS, CFCHS | Steel for Life Blue Book (UK), table v18 (BS EN 10365, BS EN 10056-1, BS EN 10210-2, BS EN 10219-2) | Millimitir iyo kg/m sida la daabacay |
| Plates | Parametric | Miisaanka area × 7850 kg/m³ |
| Custom | Cabbirradaada | Miisaanka area × 7850 kg/m³ |

Sida qaababka loo sawiro:

- **W shapes:** database-ka AISC ma siiso radius fillet, sidaas darteed DetailCraft wuxuu fillet u sawiraa r = kdes − tf.
- **S shapes iyo kanaalada (C, UPN):** flange-yo isku toosan oo dhumuc celceliska la daabacay, geesaha labajibbaaran (flange-ka dhabta ahi waa janjeersan).
- **Xagallada:** geesaha labajibbaaran.
- **HSS afar-geesle:** dhumucda derbiga naqshadda (0.93 × magaca, sida AISC 360-22 B4.2 oggolaaneyso) iyo radius geesta dibadda oo laba jibbaar ah.
- **HSS wareega iyo Pipe:** dhumucda derbiga naqshadda (0.93 × magaca).
- **IPE, HEA, HEB, UB, UC:** radius-ka xididka ee la daabacay.
- **PFC, EA, UEA:** geesaha labajibbaaran (miisaanka iyo area-ga la daabacay weli waxay ku jiraan radiyaasha).
- **SHS, RHS, CHS:** derbiga magaca. Geesaha kulul la dhamaystiray: dibadda 1.5 t, gudaha t. Qabow la sameeyay: dibadda 2 t, gudaha t ilaa 6 mm; 2.5 t iyo 1.5 t ilaa 10 mm; 3 t iyo 2 t wax ka sarreeya.

Xagal aan isku mid ahayn oo muraayad ah waa isla qaybta oo dhamaadkeeda la rogay, sida warshaddu u samayso gacanta ka soo horjeedda: godadkeeda, cope-yadeeda iyo gusset-keeda ayaa la socda.

Dooro qalab dhigid oo dooro cabbir si aad u aragto qotada, ballaca flange, dhumucda web iyo flange, radius-ka fillet, area (cm²), miisaanka (kg/m) iyo isha sanduuqa {{ui:Muuqaal dherer}} ee panelka {{ui:Sifooyinka}}.

## Waxa qaab kasta samayn karo

| Qaabka | Godadka | Cope-yada | Saxanada dhamaadka iyo saldhigga | Faylka NC | Naqshadda warshadda |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qaabka I (W, S, IPE, HEA, HEB, UB, UC, I gaar ah) | Web, flange sare, flange hoose | Haa | Saxanada dhamaadka: biim iyo tiir. Saxanada saldhigga: haa | Haa | Haa |
| Kanaalo (C, UPN, PFC) | Web, flange sare, flange hoose | Maya | Saxanada saldhigga oo kaliya | Haa | Haa |
| HSS afar-geesle, SHS, RHS | Derbiga dhinaca (web), sare, hoose | Maya | Saxanada saldhigga oo kaliya | Haa | Haa |
| HSS wareega, Pipe, CHS | Weli ma jiro | Maya | Saxanada saldhigga oo kaliya | Haa | Haa |
| Xagallo (L, EA, UEA) | Weli ma jiro | Maya | Saxanada saldhigga oo kaliya | Haa | Haa |
| Saxanno iyo bir fidsan gaar ah | Wajiga saxanka | Maya | Maya | Haa | Haa |

Godad ku jira wajiga "web" ee HSS afar-geesle wuxuu marayaa labada derbi ee dhinacyada. Waxa fayl kasta oo NC ku jiro waxaa ku qoran [Goobaha faylka NC](topic:nc1-fields).
