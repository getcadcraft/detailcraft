Jaranjaro bir toos ah: laba stringer (channel-yo sanka ku jira ama flat bar-yo kor loo taagay) iyo saxan tallaabo kasta, oo ku laxaamaysan labada dhinac. DetailCraft wuxuu xisaabiyaa tirada korka ah laga bilaabo korka, wuxuuna ku barbardhigaa xadka xeerka dhismaha, wuxuuna jaranjarada ka dhigaa hal isku-dhis oo dukaan ah.

## Dhig jaranjaro

1. Dooro {{ui:Jaranjaro}} kooxda {{ui:Dhig}}, ama riix {{key:S}}.
2. Dejintu waxay ku jirtaa midig: **jihada korista** (0° wuxuu u kacaa dhinaca +x, 90° dhinaca +y), **kor** (0 = ilaa dabaqa xiga), **socodka**, **ballaca nadiifka ah** ee u dhexeeya stringer-yada, **korka ugu sareeya ee la ogol yahay**, **stringer-ka**, **dhumucda saxanka tallaabada**, **hareerka** iyo agabka.
3. Guji isgoyn grid: taasi waa saldhigga korka koowaad, dhexda khadka dhexe ee jaranjarada.

Bar-ka xaaladda wuxuu sheegayaa inta korka ee uu sameeyay. Dooro jaranjarada (guji stringer ama tallaabo) si aad u beddesho dejinteeda, booskeeda ama phase-keeda; sifooyinka waxay sidoo kale muujinayaan korka, tallaabooyinka, socodka, pitch-ka iyo 2 × kor + socod.

## Waxa DetailCraft xisaabiyo

- **Korka.** Sida ugu yar ee korku ka hooseeyo ugu badnaan la ogol yahay: kor 3000 mm ah oo ka yar ama la mid ah 178 mm wuxuu siinayaa 17 kor oo 176.5 mm ah. Waxaa jira hal tallaabo oo ka yar korka (dabaqa sare waa tallaabada ugu dambeysa).
- **Tallaabooyinka.** Saxan socod + hareer qoto-dheer (280 + 25 = PL6x305 caadi ahaan) oo dherer ballaca nadiifka ah, sare kasta oo kor ah, geeska hore (hareerka) hortiisa khadka korka.
- **Stringers.** Pitch-ka jaranjarada, laga bilaabo meesha ay la kulmaan dabaqa hoose ilaa meesha ay la kulmaan dabaqa sare, dhinac ka baxsan ballaca nadiifka ah, geeskooda sare oo 50 mm ka sarreeya khadka hareerka. Sanka channel-ka waxay u jeedaan jaranjarada gudaheeda; flat bar sida PL12x250 waxay taagan tahay dhinac.
- **Laxaamado.** Tallaabo kasta labada stringer. Tallaabooyinka iyo stringer-ka midig waxaa lagu laxaamaa stringer-ka bidix, sidaas darteed jaranjarada wuxuu helaa hal calaamad isku-dhis (ST1) iyo naqshadda isku-dhiskiisa gaarka ah; stringers-ka waxay helaan calaamadaha qaybta st…, tallaabooyinka p….
- **Hubinta.** Digniinno marka korku ka baxo 4 ilaa 7 in. (102 ilaa 178 mm) ama socodku ka hooseeyo 11 in. (279 mm), xadadka IBC 2021 qaybta 1011.5.2; marka 2 × kor + socod uu ka baxo 600 ilaa 650 mm (xeer raaxo caadi ah); iyo marka stringer-ku uu aad uga yar yahay inuu qaado tallaabooyinka. Stringer profile aan la aqoon ayaa joojiya dhismaha jaranjarada.

## Waxa wanaagsan in la og yahay

- Dhaqdhaqaaqa, wareegga, muraayadda, nuqulka, ku dhejinta, tirtirka iyo phases-ka waxay ku shaqeeyaan jaranjarada sida xubin. Wareeg wuxuu leexiyaa kor; muraayad wuxuu u diraa dhinac kale.
- Jaranjarada caadiga ah waa hal kursi oo leh tallaabo saxan ah. Dhul-fasaxyada, gacan-qabsiga leh balusters, tallaabooyinka qallooca ah ama setka, xeerka aad doorato, liiska jarista iyo naqshadaha godadka stringer-ka, calaamee {{ui:Isticmaal dhisaha jaranjarada birta}} qalabka Stair (eeg Dhisaha jaranjarada birta).
- Stringers-ka IFC ayaa loo dhoofiyaa sida IfcMember, tallaabooyinkana sida IfcPlate; labaduba waxay tagaan faylalka NC iyo liiska alaabta. [Hubinta isku dhaca](topic:clash-check) ma taabasho qaybaha jaranjarada (waa lagu laxaamay).
- Stringers-ka waxaa lagu gooyaa toos; gooyooyinka dhamaadkooda ee dabaqyada ma la sameynayo.
- DetailCraft ma naqshadeeyo jaranjarada: stringers-ka iyo tallaabooyinka lama hubiyo adkaynta ama hoos-u-dhicidda.
