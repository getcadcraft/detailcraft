Iningi lezinqatshelwe lichazwa ebhalweni lesimo ngaphansi kwesikrini, noma ethebhini {{ui:Izinkinga}}. Zifunde kuqala. Imilayezo engezansi icashunwe njengoba iboniswa yi-DetailCraft.

## Ukubuka kwe-3D akukho

**"Lesi sikhangisi sehluleke ukuqala izinhlobo ze-3D (WebGL). Imodeli, imibiko nokuthumela kusasebenza."**

Isiphequluli sehlulekile ukuqala i-WebGL, izinhlobo ze-3D i-DetailCraft ezidweba ngazo.

1. Buyekeza isiphequluli kuzo inguqulo yakamuva.
2. Vula ukusheshisa kwehadwe bese uqala kabusha isiphequluli (bheka ngezansi).
3. Buyekeza umshayeli wegraphics, noma zama esinye isiphequluli. Ama-desktop akude nemishini ezingeniswe kwesinye ngokuvamile azinayo i-WebGL.
4. Okwamanje ungasasebenza: beka izitshalo ngefomu {{ui:Engeza ngegridi}}, funda imibiko, uthumele. Ukuchofoza ngaphakathi kokubuka kudinga i-WebGL kuphela.

:::chromium
Ukuvula ukusheshisa kwehadwe, vula **Izilungiselelo, Isistimu** bese uvula **Sebenzisa ukusheshisa kwegraphics uma kutholakala**, bese uqala kabusha.
:::

:::firefox
Ukuvula ukusheshisa kwehadwe, vula **Izilungiselelo, Okujwayelekile, Ukusebenza**, susa ukukhethwa kwe-**Sebenzisa izilungiselelo zokusebenza ezincomekayo** bese ukhetha **Sebenzisa ukusheshisa kwehadwe uma kutholakala**. Qala kabusha i-Firefox.
:::

:::safari
I-Safari inayo i-WebGL ivuliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Buyekeza i-macOS uma ukubuka kungasaqali, bese uhlola ukuthi akukho sandiso esiyivimbelayo.
:::

Okwengeziwe ku-[Izisakazi nezikhompyutha](common:browsers).

## Ukuchofoza ngaphakathi kokubuka akwenzi lutho

- **Akukho ukuhlangana eduze nokuchofoza.** Amathuluzi okubeka ahlanganisa kuphela ezinhlanganweni zegridi. Ukuchofoza kude nawo wonke amachashaza akwenzi lutho. Sondela kuze kube yilapho ichashaza likhanyisa.
- **Ithuluzi lingu-{{ui:Khetha}} bese uchofoza isikhala esingenalutho.** Lokho kusula ukukhethwa.
- **Udonse.** Ukucindezela nokukhipha okude ngamapikseli ambalwa kuba yindonso: iphendula noma ihambisa ukubuka.
- **Ingxenye isikiwe.** Ku-{{ui:Ipulani}} ne-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}, insimbi ngaphandle kokusika iyafihlwa futhi ayikwazi ukuchofozwa. Shintsha uye ku-{{ui:3D}}.
- **Ukuhlangana kulezinga elingalungile.** Ku-{{ui:3D}}, izinhlanganiso ezinamazinga amaningi zingahlangana esikrinini; ukuchofoza kukhetha eyeseduze kakhulu. Sebenzisa i-{{ui:Ipulani}} noma i-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}.

## Izixhumi azakhi

### "Lesi bhimu asiphelelanga umugqa wensika."

Amapuleti okuphela adinga umphetho webhimu ube ngqo emgqeni wensika eqondile, phakathi kwawo ezansi nakwawo phezulu (ngaphakathi kwe-1 mm). Hlola i-{{ui:Imiphetho (x, y, z mm)}} yebhimu nendawo yensika.

### "Lesi bhimu sihlangana nowebhu wensika noma ngokutshekile"

Noma ku-{{ui:Izinkinga}}: "Amabhimu ahlangana nowebhu wensika awakakhiwa: phendula insika ngama-90° noma sebenzisa oxhumo olunye."

Ibhimu lingena ngowebhu wensika. Khetha insika bese usetha {{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}} ibe ngu-90, bese uzama futhi. Okuhlanganisiwe okuthambekile akwakhiwa.

### "Amapuleti okuphela akhiwa amabhimu aphansi kuphela (leli bhimu liyathambeka)."

Izinhlangothi zombili zebhimu kufanele zibe ngaphezulu kwesinye.

### "Amapuleti okuphela adinga ibhimu eliqondile (ukujikeleza 0)."

Buyisela i-{{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}} webhimu ibe ngu-0.

### "Amapuleti okuphela adinga ibhimu le-I-shape." / "Amapuleti okuphela adinga insika ye-I-shape."

Kuphela ama-I-shape (W, IPE, HEA, HEB noma i-I yezifiso) athatha amapuleti okuphela.

### "Umphetho webhimu awusaphelele emgqeni wensika."

Uwuhambisile ibhimu noma insika ngemva kokungeza ixhumo. Buyisela, noma susa ixhumo.

### "Ixhumo lisike lesi sakhiwo laba yize."

Izixhumi emaphethelweni womabili zisike isakhiwo emuva ngaphezu kobude baso, isibonelo ibhimu elifushane kakhulu phakathi kwezinsika ezijulile. Hlola imiphetho yesakhiwo nokuqina kwephleyiti.

### "Ayikho eminye imihlanganiso efana nale … engenaxhumo."

{{ui:Okufanayo}} ikopisha kuphela kumahlanganiso anephrofayili efanayo yebhimu, iphrofayili efanayo yensika nohlobo lwexhumo olufanayo, ibhimu libe iflenji, angabi nexhumo.

### "Omabili amaphetho sesevele enezixhumi." / "Le nsika sesivele inepuleti lesisekelo."

Khetha uxhumo olukhona ukuze ulushintshe, noma uqale ususe.

## Izexwayiso ethebhini le-Izinkinga

Izexwayiso azivimbi ixhumo noma ukusekelwa ukuthi kwakhiwe; zikucela ukuthi uhlole. Izexwayiso zemiphetho nezohlelo zisebenzisa okuncane kwe-EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.3 (umphetho 1.2 × imbobo, ukuhamba 2.2 × imbobo, ububanzi 2.4 × imbobo). Uma iphrojekthi yakho isebenzisa imithetho ehlukile, isexwayiso singase singasebenzi; i-DetailCraft ayiklami izixhumi.

"ingxenye yembobo ingaphandle kwemphetho wengxenye" kusho ukuthi i-x yembobo ingaphansi kuka-0 noma ingaphezu kobude besikiwe. Khumbula ukuthi i-x isuka ekuqaleni kokusika, ngemva kokuthi izixhumi zisike ingxenye.

## Okokulanda akuvelayo

Konke ukulondoloza nokuthumela kuba ukulandwa kwesiphequluli. Hlola uhlu lokulanda lwesiphequluli sakho. Uma isiphequluli sibuza ngaphambi kokulanda amafayela amaningi, noma sivimbela ukulanda kule sayithi, vumela. Ku-Safari, hlola inkinobho yokulanda ebhareni yamathuluzi.

## Okhiye abazi lutho

- Ikhesa lisendaweni yombhalo noma yenombolo: amathuluzi wokhiye kanye no{{key:Delete}} ayanakwa lapho ukuze ukwazi ukubhala. Cindezela {{key:Tab}} noma uchofoze ukubuka.
- Kukhona ingxoxo evuliwe: yivale kuqala.
- Isiphequluli sinokhiye (isibonelo ibha yekheli): chofoza ngaphakathi kwe-DetailCraft.
- {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} isebenzisa i-Control noma ku-Mac.

## Izinkinga zokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi

Inkinobho {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ebhareni lesicelo ikhombisa isimo sekopi yangaphandle. Ukuthi ukulondoloza kusebenza kanjani: [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

| Inkinobho ifunda | Okusho | Yenzani |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} | Akukho kopi yangaphandle kwe-inthanethi kulesi sisakazi okwamanje | Yikhethe uma uxhumeke |
| Ilondoloza 5 kwezingu-20 | Ukulondolozwa sekuqhubeka | Linda {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}; gcina ithebhu ivulekile |
| {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} | I-DetailCraft, ukubuka kwayo kwe-3D, wonke amathumelo nalo usizo kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhuma | Akukho lutho. Chofoza ukuze {{ui:Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo entsha kakhulu)}} noma {{ui:Susa ikopi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} |
| {{ui:Akukwazanga ukulondoloza}} | Amanye amafayela ahlehlanga ekulandeni | Hlola uxhumo bese uchofoza ukuze uzame futhi |
| {{ui:Ayikho ingaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} | Lesi sisakazi asikwazi ukugcina ikopi yangaphandle: amawindi angasese nezinye izikhompyutha ezilawulwayo ziyayivimbela | Sebenzisa iwindi elijwayelekile, noma sebenza ku-inthanethi |

Uma usungaphandle kwe-inthanethi futhi inkinobho ingafundi i-{{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}, ungayilayishi kabusha noma uvale ithebhu: lokho okulayishiwe kusaqhubeka nokusebenza. Londoloza imodeli yakho nge-{{key:Mod+S}} beseulinda uze uxhumeke futhi.

## Umsebenzi ongakalondolozwa

Uma isiphequluli sibuza ukuthi ufuna ukushiya ikhasi, i-DetailCraft inoshintsho olungakalondolozwa. Khetha ukuhlala, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+S}}. I-DetailCraft ayikwazi ukubuyisa imodeli ngemva kokuvalwa kwethebhu.
