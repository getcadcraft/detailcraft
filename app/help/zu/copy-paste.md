Iqembu elithi {{ui:Hlela}} ku-ithulusi linezinto ezithi {{ui:Hlehlisa}}, {{ui:Phinda wenze}}, {{ui:Kopisha}} no-{{ui:Namathisela}}. Imenyu yalo ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} iqukethe okunye: {{ui:Sika}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}, {{ui:Phinda}}, {{ui:Susa}}, {{ui:Khetha konke}} no-{{ui:Khetha lutho}}, ngalinye nokhiye walo. Inkinobho noma into yemenyu engenakwenza okuthile manje ibonakala ingasebenzi; hlanganisa phezu kwayo ukuze ufunde isizathu (isibonelo "akukakopishwa lutho").

Yonke into yokunamathisela, yokusika, yokuphinda nokususa iyisinyathelo esisodwa: ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kanye kubuyisela konke emuva.

## Khetha izingxenye eziningi

- Chofoza ingxenye ukuze uyikhethe. Bamba {{key:Shift}} (noma {{key:Ctrl}}, noma {{key:Mod}}) bese uchofoza ezinye izingxenye ukuze uzengeze; chofoza eyikhethiwe ngendlela efanayo ukuze uyisuse.
- {{key:Mod+A}} noma {{ui:Khetha konke}} ikhetha wonke amalunga, izixhumanisi nezisekelo.
- {{key:Esc}} noma {{ui:Khetha lutho}} isula ukukhethwa.

Uma kukhethwe izinto eziningi, ipanele le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} ibhalwe ngokuthi "4 ikhethiwe" (noma yiluphi inani) futhi libala amalunga ngohlobo, izixhumanisi, izisekelo kanye nesisindo sensimbi esiphelele, nezinkinobho ezithi {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Phinda}} no-{{ui:Susa}}.

## Kopisha

1. Khetha amalunga nezisekelo ofuna ukuwakopisha.
2. Khetha {{ui:Kopisha}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+C}}.

Okuhamba nakho:

- Ilunga ngalinye liphelele: iphrofayili, impahla, ukuphela, indawo, ukujikeleza, igama, izimbobo ne-cope. Iphrofayili yezifiso eyisebenzisayo nayo iyakopishwa.
- Isisekelo ngasinye siphelele: usayizi, ukhonkolo, insika esezansi kanye nezinduku zokuqala.
- Amapuleti okuphela namapuleti esisekelo, kuphela uma wonke ilunga axhunywa yisixhumanisi lakopishwa: ipuleti lokuphela lebhimu lidinga ibhimu nensika yalo. Ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi izixhumanisi ezingaki zishiyiwe. Ukushiya izixhumanisi ngamabomu, susa ukukhetha okuthi **Kopisha amapuleti okuphela namapuleti esisekelo namalunga awo** ku-[izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings).

Ixhumanisi elodwa lodwa alikwazi ukukopishwa; khetha amalunga awo. Ukunikeza okunye ukuhlangana ixhumanisi elifanayo, sebenzisa {{ui:Okufanayo}} (bheka [Amapuleti okuphela](topic:end-plates#copy-it-to-every-similar-joint)).

Ikhophi ihlala kule bhrawuza kuze kukopishwe okunye. Enye ithebhu noma iwindi le-DetailCraft kule bhrawuza ingayinamathisela, ngakho ungakopisha uhlaka kwemodeli eyodwa bese uluntamathisela kwenye.

## Namathisela ohlanganweni lwegridi

1. Khetha {{ui:Namathisela}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+V}}.
2. Hambisa ipoyinta ngaphezu kombono. Umugqa ogxilile wekhophi ulandela uhlangano lwegridi oluseduze kakhulu nepoyinta.
3. Chofoza uhlangano. Iphoyinti lesisekelo lekhophi (ikhona eliphansi kakhulu: i-x, y no-z ezincane kakhulu kukho konke okukopishiwe) lifika lapho.

Izingxenye ezinamathisiwe zikhethwa ngemva kwalokho, ukuze ukwazi ukunamathisela futhi kwenye indawo, noma ukuzishintsha. {{key:Esc}} ikhansela ukunamathisela; ikhophi ihlala ebhodini lokukopisha. {{ui:Ipulani}} no-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} benza kube lula ukufinyelela uhlangano olufanele.

:::tip
Ukuphinda ibay, khetha amalunga ayo (noma konke ngo-{{key:Mod+A}}), kopisha, namathisela, bese uchofoza uhlangano oluya ngakwesokudla ibay eyodwa kusuka lapho kukhona eliphansi kakhulu lekhophi.
:::

## Namathisela ngekhibhodi

Ngesikhathi unamathisela, ipanele le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} likhombisa okusebhodini lokukopisha nezimo ezimbili:

- {{ui:Namathisela ngokuphambuka (mm, kusuka kwoyisekela)}}: thayipha x, y no-z bese ukhetha {{ui:Namathisela kulokhu kuphambuka}}. Iqala ngokuphambuka kusuka kuthi [izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings).
- {{ui:Namathisela ohlanganweni lwegridi}}: khetha igridi enamagama, igridi enezinombolo nezinga, bese ukhetha {{ui:Namathisela lapha}}. Iphoyinti lesisekelo liya lapho.

## Namathisela endaweni

{{ui:Namathisela endaweni}} ({{key:Mod+Shift+V}}) ibeka ikhophi ngqo lapho ikopishwe khona. Yisebenzise ngemva kwe-{{ui:Sika}}, noma ukuletha izingxenye kwenye imodeli endaweni efanayo. Uma imvelo isasekhona, ikhophi ilele phezu kwazo futhi ibha yesimo iyakuxwayisa: hambisa amakhophi ngo-{{ui:Ukuqala}} no-{{ui:Ukuphela}} ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}, noma yenza kabuyele emuva.

## Phinda

{{ui:Phinda}} ({{key:Mod+D}}) ikopisha ukukhethwa bese iyinamathisela ngokushesha, ihanjiswe ngokuphambuka okusezilungiselelweni ze-DetailCraft (ngo-6000 mm ecaleni kwe-x ekuqaleni). Ayishintshi okusebhodini lokukopisha. Amakhophi akhethwa ngemva kwalokho, ngakho ukucindezela {{key:Mod+D}} futhi kwenza elilandelayo kuwo.

## Sika nokususa

- {{ui:Sika}} ({{key:Mod+X}}) ikopisha ukukhethwa, bese iyasusa. Yinamathisele lapho kufanele khona.
- {{ui:Susa}} ({{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}}) isusa ukukhethwa. Ukususa ilunga kususa nezixhumanisi zalo.

## Amamaki ngemva kokunamathisela

Amamaki wengxenye nawezinhlanganisela awagcinwa; i-DetailCraft iwabala kabusha kusuka kumodeli ngemva kwawo wonke amashintshi ([Indlela amamaki anikezwa ngayo](topic:mark-rules)). Ngakho:

- Ikhophi efana ngempela nomthombo wayo (iphrofayili, ibanga, ubude obusikiwe, izimbobo ne-cope ezifanayo) yabelana ngemaki yomthombo, futhi inani kwi-{{ui:Amamaki wengxenye}} nasemshweni wezinto liyanda.
- Ikhophi ehlukile ekugcineni ithola imaki yayo. Isibonelo, ibhimu elinamathiselwe ngaphandle kwamapuleti alo okuphela aliswa ngemuva, ngakho liyinto ehlukile. Amamaki abalwa kabusha kuyo yonke imodeli, ngakho nezinombolo zezinye izingxenye zingashintsha.

Wonke ilunga, imbobo, i-cope, ixhumanisi kanye nesisekelo esinamathisiwe kuthola i-id entsha, ngakho akukho okwabelanwa emodelini phakathi kwekhophi nomthombo wayo.

## Uma ibhodi lokukopisha lenqabile

Okusebhodini lokukopisha kuhlolwa ngokucophelela njengefayela lemodeli olivulayo: noma yini engeyona ingxenye evumelekile ye-DetailCraft iyalahlwa, futhi ibha yesimo ithi kuthini. Ibhodi lokukopisha elifakwe yi-DetailCraft entsha, noma into enye, lenqatshwa ngomyalezo futhi akukho okunamathiselwa. Uma iwindi eliyimfihlo lingakwazi ukugcina ikhophi kusitoreji sebhrawuza, ikhophi isasebenza kuleyo thebhu.
