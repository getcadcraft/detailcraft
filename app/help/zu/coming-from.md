Uma uhlela nge-Tekla Structures, iningi le-DetailCraft liyakubonakala lijwayelekile: amagridi, amazinga, amaprofayili avela ekhathalogu, izingxenye namahlanganisela, uxhumo, ukunumbera, amafayela e-NC, i-IFC. Usayizi uhlukile kakhulu: i-Tekla Structures iyisistimu ephelele yokukhiqiza, kanti i-DetailCraft iyi-app encane, esaqala yesiphequluli eyenza imisebenzi embalwa kusukela ekuqaleni kuya ekupheleni. Leli khasi likutshela lapho izinkambiso zakho zidlulela khona nalapho zingadluli khona.

:::tip
Uma izandla zakho zazi indandatho yeTekla Structures, khetha isitayela semenyu {{ui:Indandatho yensimbi}} ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:DetailCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}: amathebhu i-Modeling, Detailing, Edit, Drawings, Fabrication kanye ne-View, nenkinobho ephakathi iyashelelisa. Uma uvela ku-AutoCAD futhi uthanda ukubhala imiyalo, i-{{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}} yengeza umugqa womyalo (BEAM, COLUMN, ENDPLATE, ZOOM …). Bona [Imenyu, okhiye nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).
:::

## Umehluko okwehlisa abantu

### Amaphawu ashintsha ngokwawo

Akukho sinyathelo sokunumbera. Amaphawu abalwa kabusha ngemva kwalokho konke oshintshayo, ngakho asoze aphele, kepha futhi angabuye anumbere kabusha: engeza insika futhi i-c3 yayiphethwe izolo ingase manje ibe yi-c4. Akukho "umlando wokunumbera" wokugcina amaphawu adala. Qeda imodeli, bese wenza i-BOM, amafayela e-NC nemizobo yokukhiqiza ndawonye kusuka kufayela elilodwa eligciniwe.

### Inkinobho ephakathi yesilwane iyasondeza; ayishelelisi

Kumbono we-3D: ukudonsa ngakwesobunxele kuyajikeleza, ukudonsa ngakwesokudla kuyashelelisa, insimbi yevili iyasondeza, nokudonsa ngenkinobho ephakathi nakho kuyasondeza. Ukubamba {{key:Shift}} ngenkathi udonsa ngakwesobunxele nakho kuyashelelisa. Ivili lisondeza emaphakathini ombono, hhayi kuphoyinti lesikhomba. Akukho ukukhetha isikhungo sokujikeleza: imodeli ijikeleza emaphakathini ombono. Bona [Hambela kuzungeza umbono we-3D](topic:navigate-3d).

### Amakholomu ngokuzenzakalelayo abheka endleleni eyodwa

Ku-rotation 0 ama-flange ensika abheka ohlangothini lwegridi enombolo. Amabhimu adlula phakathi kwemigqa yegridi yezinhlamvu afaka kumweb, futhi amapuleti okuphela ayanqatshelwa lapho ("Amabhimu afaka kwi-web yensika akakakwakhiwa"). Zungeza lezo zinsika ngama-90° ngaphambi kokuthi wengeze uxhumo.

### Ukubukeka kohlangothi kumigqa yezinhlamvu kuphela

{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} ibheka emigqeni yegridi yezinhlamvu kuphela. Akukho mibono emigqeni enombolo, akukho mibono egciniwe futhi akukho amaphlani okusebenza. Sebenzisa {{ui:3D}} noma {{ui:Engeza ngegridi}} ngefreyimu emigqeni enombolo.

### Ukukhetha, ukukopisha nokuhambisa

Khetha izingxenye eziningi nge-{{key:Shift}}+chofoza noma {{key:Mod+A}}, bese ukopisha, unqamule, unamathisele (ehlanganweni lwegridi, ngokushintshwa okuthayipwe, noma endaweni) futhi uphindaphinde, kubandakanya nezixhumo ([Kopisha, namathisela futhi uphindaphinde](topic:copy-paste)). Akukho ukukhetha ngefasitela, akukho sisefa sokukhetha, akukho ukuhambisa, ukubuyisa esibukweni noma ukuhlela ngamaqoqo, kanti izakhiwo zishintshwa elunga ngalinye ephaneli ethi {{ui:Izakhiwo}}.

### Amagridi awadonsi izinto nazo

Izinto zigcina izixhumanisi zazo uma ushintsha ukuhlukaniswa kwegridi; akukho okuhamba nemigqa yegridi. Shintsha igridi ngaphambi kokubeka insimbi, noma uhlele izixhumanisi zokuphela kwelunga ngalinye kamuva.

### Uxhumo aluklanywa

Lezi zixhumo ezimbili zibeka amapuleti, imigodi, amabhawodi nokushisela ngokusho izinombolo onikezayo. Akukho ukuhlolwa kokuklama kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo, kuphela izexwayiso zomkhawulo nesikhala (ezivela ku-EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.3).

### Imigodi ayinqanyulwa kumbono we-3D

Imigodi ibonakala njengamapulagi amnyama. Yimigodi ephelele kumafayela e-NC, kwimizobo yokukhiqiza nakufayela le-IFC. Ukuqoshwa kunqanyulwa kumbono.

### Izisindo zingokuphelele

Isisindo sensimbi asikhiphi imigodi noma ukuqoshwa, nesisindo samabhawodi asifakiwe.

### Kuphela amamillimitha

Konke kuthayipwa kubonakale ngamamillimitha, nakumaphrofayili e-AISC. Amagama amaphrofayili agcina ifomu alishicilelwe kulo (W12x50, HSS6x6x1/4).

### Akukho imizobo yokuphatha

Akukho uhlu lwemizobo, akukho umhleli wemizobo, akukho imizobo yokuhlelwa jikelele noma yokuhlanganisa. Umdwebo wokukhiqiza uyenziwa lapho uwucela, bese uyalandwa; ukuwushintsha, shintsha imodeli bese uyawenza kabusha.

### Isebenza kusiphequluli

Ukugcina kulanda ifayela. Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi esiphequluli sizigcinela zona azisebenzi. Amamodeli akho akusoze aphume ekhompyutheni yakho: bona [Ukuthi amafayela akho aya kuphi](common:your-files).

## Okufanele uqhubeke ukukwenza esofthiwe yakho yamanje

Namuhla i-DetailCraft ifanele amafreyimu amancane, ukuhlola umqondo, ukufundisa, noma ukukhiqiza amafayela e-NC nemizobo yokukhiqiza yomsebenzi olula wamabhimu nezinsika. Gcina uxhumo oluyinkimbinkimbi, imizobo yokuhlelwa jikelele, imizobo yokumisa, amaphrojekthi amaningi-msebenzisi nanoma yini ehlu ku-[Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet) kusofthiwe oyisebenzisayo manje.
