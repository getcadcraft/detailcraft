Kwiqembu le-{{ui:Okukhiphayo}}, vula {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Ifayela le-KISS lesofthiwe yokukhiqiza}}. I-DetailCraft ilanda `<name>.kss`, ifayela le-KISS ("Keep It Simple, Steel", inguqulo 1.1), ifomethi yombhalo osobala ezinhlelweni eziningi zokuphatha ukukhiqiza ezingenisa uhlu lwempahla kuzo.

## Okuqukethwe

- Ikhanda: inombolo yomsebenzi (izinhlamvu nezinombolo eziyisishiyagalombili zokuqala zegama lemodeli), igama lemodeli, usuku nesikhathi, nokuthi usayizi ungabe usemetric yini.
- Ngamaka ngamaka we-assembly: ingxenye yawo eyinhloko (inani = ama-assembly angaki), umugqa womdwebo (A-B1 njalo njalo, inombolo yomdwebo we-assembly), umugqa we-assembly (igama, uhlobo, isisindo, ubude jikelele, ukujula nobubanzi), umugqa wokulandelana ngesigaba ngasinye i-assembly kuso, kanye nemigqa yomsebenzi: izimbobo (zingaki, ububanzi, ukuqhubeka ngobukhulu), ama-cope namawelda.
- Bese kuba ingxenye ngayinye exhunyiwe kuyo, nenani **eliphelele** (ngokwama-assembly × ama-assembly), njengoba umgomo ufuna.
- Amabhawodi akhululekile namandla okubopha ku-drawing BOLTS, ne-assembly FIELD.
- Umugqa ngamunye unikeza uhlobo (W, C, L, HSS, PL, noma MI weprofayili yezifiso), usayizi, igreyidi, ubude bokusika ngamamilimitha, igama lengxenye nesisindo.

## Imperial noma metric

Kuzilungiselelo ze-[DetailCraft](topic:settings), **Usayizi wamafayela e-KISS** ukhetha:

- **Okuzenzakalelayo** (okokuqala): imperial uma wonke amalungu eyizakhiwo ze-AISC, metric uma kungenjalo.
- **Imperial**: izakhiwo ngesibizo sazo se-AISC (W 16X26, HSS 4X4X.250); amapuleti namabhawodi, ayi-DetailCraft ayagcina ngamamilimitha, aseduze ne-1/16 in; izisindo ngamapawundi.
- **Metric**: izakhiwo zaseYurophu ngegama lazo (IPE300), amapuleti njengobukhulu X ububanzi ngamamilimitha; izisindo ngamakhilogremu.

Ubude buhlala bungamamilimitha ngamadesimali amabili, noma kunjani isilungiselelo: umgomo we-KISS uyakubiza lokho, nohlelo olwamukelayo luyabaguqula.

:::note
Amagreyidi asikwa abe izinhlamvu eziyisishiyagalombili zomgomo: A572 Gr 50 iba yi-A572-50. Amakhoma emagameni aba izikhala (ifomethi ayinakho ukuvalwa ngezimpawu). Hlola ukungeniswa okukodwa ohlelweni lwakho ngaphambi kokuthembela kukho: amafayela e-KISS e-DetailCraft ahlolwa ngomgomo lapha, hhayi ohlelweni lokukhiqiza oluthile okwamanje.
:::
