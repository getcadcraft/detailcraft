Lokhu kwe-shintsha ku-DetailCraft 0.9, uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.8.1. I-0.9 iletha usayizi wensimbi yase-UK, umdwebo wokukhiqiza wengxenye ngayinye ngesikhathi esisodwa, ithebhu ye-{{ui:Analysis}} enkulu yezinhlaka, nokubona amakhesa abanye lapho nisebenza ndawonye.

## Okusha

### Insimbi yase-UK nemidwebo yokukhiqiza

- **Usayizi wensimbi yase-UK.** Izinhlu zamaprofayili manje zinosayizi abayi-976 ovela ku-Steel for Life Blue Book: UB, UC, PFC, EA, UEA, CHS, SHS kanye ne-RHS, kanti amasekhishini ebandayo ahlukaniswa ngokwahlukana (amagama awo aqala nge-CF). Amagama abhalwa umndeni kuqala, njengoba kunjalo ku-UB305x165x40 noma ku-SHS100x100x5.0, futhi nokubhala njengoba imidwebo ikubhala kuyasebenza: 305x165x40 UB, L100x100x10, SHS100x5. Bona [Uhlu lwamaprofayili](topic:profile-catalog).
- **Ama-angle angalingani.** Ama-angle e-UEA agcina zombili izingalo ngokudlula ku-IFC ngaphakathi nangaphandle nasemafayelweni e-NC, futhi ukubonisa kuphendula isicucu, siphele ngalapho siqale.
- **Yonke imidwebo yokukhiqiza ngesikhathi esisodwa.** Khetha {{ui:Shop drawing}} kweqembu le-{{ui:Output}} ngaphandle kokukhethwa lutho. Iwindi lidwebela uhlu lwazo zonke izimaka zengxenye, lubonise ukubuka kwanoma imuphi kuzo, futhi {{ui:Download all}} ilondoloza i-PDF eyodwa enekhasi ngomaka ngamunye, ngamunye unesihloko esithi "Ishidi 3 kwangu-42". {{ui:Download this sheet}} ilondoloza eli obheka kulo. Amachannel, ama-angle namasekhishini aphakathi nawo manje athola amashidi. Bona [Yenza imidwebo yokukhiqiza](topic:shop-drawings).
- **Iziphrofayili zami.** Iphrofayili yezifiso manje ingagcinwa kuwo wonke imodeli entsha. Kwifomu le-Custom profile, shiya {{ui:Keep in My profiles}} inomakiwe; uhlu lwe-{{ui:My profiles}} oluphansi kwawo lunezinkinobho {{ui:Keep}}, {{ui:Use here}} kanye ne-{{ui:Remove}}. Uhlu lugcinwa kulesi siphequluli (kuze kube ngu-200), hhayi ku-akhawunti yakho. Bona [Amapuleti namaprofayili](topic:plates-and-profiles).

### Ukuhlaziya

- **Okwesibili (P-Delta).** Makha {{ui:Second order (P-Delta)}} eduze kwe-Qalisa ethebhini ethi {{ui:Analysis}} bese amandla e-axial asebenza ohlakeni olugobile. Amapuleti namabhulokhi aqinile asala ngokwesigaba sokuqala, kanye nesixwayiso. Bona [Amandla, izimpendulo nokugoba](topic:frame-analysis).
- **Amafrikhwensi wemvelo.** Ibhulokhi ethi {{ui:Finite elements: natural frequencies}} ngaphansi kwemiphumela ithola izindlela eziphansi kakhulu zalo modeli ofanayo, nesikhathi nobunzima bomthwalo obusebenzayo, futhi ibonise indlela emodeli. Bona [Amandla, izimpendulo nokugoba](topic:frame-analysis).
- **Imithwalo yomoya nendawo yokunyakaza komhlaba.** Amabhulokhi amathathu amasha ethebhini ethi {{ui:Analysis}}: {{ui:Wind loads (ASCE 7 directional procedure)}}, {{ui:Seismic site and system (ASCE 7)}} kanye ne-{{ui:ASCE 7 load combinations}}. Bona [Umoya, indawo yokunyakaza komhlaba nezinhlanganiso ze-ASCE 7](topic:wind-seismic-combinations).

### Ukusebenza ndawonye

- **Amakhesa namagama.** Eseshini ephilayo, ipointa yomuntu ngamunye idwetshwa kwimodeli ngegama lakhe nombala wakhe, nalokho akukhethile kuthola ibhokisi.
- **Landela umuntu.** Vula {{ui:People}} eseshini bese ukhetha {{ui:Follow}} egameni; ukubuka kwakho kuhamba nokwakhe. Umphathi weseshini angakhetha {{ui:Ask everyone to follow me}}. Bona [Landela umuntu eseshini ephilayo](topic:follow-in-live).

### Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- Ama-tooltip angama-Bootstrap, ikhadi lethiphu lokuqala linenkinobho yokuvala, imigqa yamathebhu iyaskrola ngecala uma ingalingani, nethebhu yesiphequluli iqamba idokhumenti evulekile ilandelwe ngo-DetailCraft no-CadCraft.

## Kulungisiwe

- Iphrofayili yezifiso yayilahleka uma uqala imodeli entsha.
- Amachannel, ama-angle namasekhishini aphakathi kwakungekho esethini yemidwebo yokukhiqiza.

## Imikhawulo eqinisekile

- Amabhulokhi omoya, wokunyakaza komhlaba kanye nezakhi eziphelele ayizisizo zokuhlola. Unjiniyela wakho ubuka imiphumela; akuyona ukuhlolwa kwekhodi. Ukuthola kwe-USGS kuhlolwe esimweni esimele, hhayi kwisevisi ephilayo.
- Izakhi eziphelele ne-P-Delta zihlolwe ngezimpendulo zesandla nezezibalo ezivaliwe, hhayi esakhiweni sangempela. Ukuhlolwa kokuhleleka (buckling) akukakwakhiwa.
- Akekho umenzi wensimbi waseUK ozame usayizi wase-UK okwamanje, futhi amafayela e-NC wama-angle angalingani awazange asebenze emshinini.
- Isethi yemidwebo yokukhiqiza iyi-PDF kuphela.
- Izilimi ngaphandle kwesiNgisi ziyizinhlobo zomshini.
- Abafakeli be-Windows abasayinwanga ikhodi (Olunye ulwazi, bese Qhubeka noma kunjalo); isithombe sediski se-macOS asiqinisekiswanga (cofa ngesokudla, bese Vula).

Ezinye izinguquko zisemanothini ka-0.8 nako-0.8.1 ezingaphansi kwalelikhasi.

## Kwangaphambili: 0.8 no-0.8.1

Lokhu kwe-shintsha ku-DetailCraft 0.8 (1 Novemba 2026) no-0.8.1 (2 Novemba 2026), uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.7. I-0.7 yahlola amalungu ensimbi; i-0.8 yahlela ukuphakanyiswa, yaphakamisa uxhumano, yahlola izisekelo nensimbi yokuqinisa, yakha izindonga zomthi nezinhlaka ezilula, ongqimba nophahla.

### Okusha

**Ukumisa**

- **Uhlelo lokumisa nemuvi.** Ethebhini ethi {{ui:Erection}}, chaza icrane (lapho imi khona, ikhoyi yayo ephezulu, kanye nokuboshwa okungekho kuchathi yomthwalo) bese uthayipha imigqa yechathi yayo yomthwalo. I-DetailCraft iphakamisa imodeli ingxenye ngayinye yesakhiwo, ngokulandelana kwezigaba nangamazinga, futhi ihlola ngayinye ukuphakanyiswa ngokuchathi ngokwebanga layo nangomkhawulo owusethile. {{ui:Save movie}} ibhala ifayela le-HTML elodwa elidlala ngaphandle kokuxhumeka kunoma yisiphi isiphequluli, nakufoni. Ukuphakanyiswa okudlula ichathi, okudlula kuyo noma okuphakeme kakhulu kukwi-{{ui:Problems}}. Kuyihlelo elivela ezinombolweni zakho, hhayi uhlelo lokuphakanyisa oluphelele. Bona [Uhlelo lokumisa nemuvi](topic:erection-movie).

**Uxhumano**

- **I-autopilot yoxhumano.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Autopilot}} ibheka wonke amahlangano angenaxhumano bese iphakamisa oyedwa ngokwezimo: amapuleti esisekelo, amathebhu okusika, amapuleti ekugcineni kwamabhimu owamaka njengamabhimu wesikhathi, izixhumi namagusethi. Nika umthwalo ofanayo noma ukusika komunye wokugcina, bese inani lama-bolt lilandela amandla okuklama e-bolt. Ukuthi {{ui:Add them all}} kwengeza amaphakamiso njengesinyathelo sinye sokuhlehlisa. Ayizinqubomgomo zokuqala zokubuka, hhayi ukuklanywa koxhumano.
- **Ukuhlola ukwakheka.** I-{{ui:Problems}} ibhala i-bolt engafinyeleleki ngesipanela, ama-bolt aseduze kakhulu nesocket, amawelda e-fillet angaphandle kwemikhawulo yosayizi wawo nezigaba ezingenayo amanzi. Ukufinyelela kokuwelda kusha: iweld e-fillet ejikelezayo engafinyeleleki yitoshi lomweldi ibhalwa kanye nengxenye eyivimba nengxenye yokuwelda ethintekayo. Bona [Ukuhlola ukwakheka](topic:constructability).

**Izisekelo nensimbi yokuqinisa**

- **Ukuhlola izisekelo.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Foundation}} ihlola ama-footing, ama-footing ahlanganisiwe namathi ngenhlabathi oyichazile: ukumelela, ukudonsela nokugoqeka ngemithwalo yesevisi; ukugoba, ukusika, ukuphunga, ukusebenza nensimbi encane ngemithwalo esetshenzisiwe (ACI 318-19). Ikholomu eseduze komphetho noma ikhona yanqunywa ngesigaba esinqunywe umphetho we-footing. Ukuncipha kuvela ku-modulus yenhlabathi, ithoni elithambile lixazululwa ngamaspring ngemodulus yomhlabathi ongaphansi, namaqembu amapayili nodonga oluqinisa nawo anamapaneli awo. Imithwalo yamakholomu ingabhalwa noma ithathwe ku-{{ui:Analysis}}. Bona [Ukuhlola izisekelo](topic:foundation-checks).
- **Imephu yokuminyana kwensimbi.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Congestion}} idweba imephu eshisayo ye-footing ngayinye njengoba ibonakala iphezulu: lapho izinti zishiya indawo encane kakhulu ukuthi ukhonkithi ehle, ngokumelene nomthetho wesikhala esicacile kanye, uma unikeza, i-aggregate enkulu kakhulu nekhanda lesidlidlizi. Bona [Imephu yokuminyana kwensimbi](topic:rebar-congestion).

**Izinhlaka nokuhlinzeka**

- **Izinhlaka zomthi nezelayiti elilula.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Framing}} yakha amapaneli wodonga, wesakhiwo nophahla ngayo yonke ingxenye, uhlu lokusika, isitoko okufanele usithenge kanye nomdwebo wokukhiqiza ngesikali. I-{{ui:Place as members}} ibeka izingxenye emodelini, futhi i-{{ui:PDF sheets}} ilondoloza ishidi elilodwa ngephaneli ngalinye. Usayizi, amabanga nama-header ngabakho: i-DetailCraft ayihloli ukuthi ayathwala umthwalo. Bona [Amapaneli ezinhlaka zomthi nezelayiti elilula](topic:framing-panels).
- **Isitoko somhlinzeki nezikhathi zokulinda.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Supply}} ifunda uhlu lwesitoko somhlinzeki (CSV) futhi ibonise, ngayinye yezigaba uhlu lokusika olufuna ukuthenga, ukuthi kukhona yini okwanele esitokweni nokuthi ukulinda kuthatha isikhathi esingakanani. Izigaba ezingaphelele nezibambezelekile zikwi-{{ui:Problems}}. Akukho okuyaliwa. Bona [Isitoko somhlinzeki nezikhathi zokulinda](topic:supplier-stock).

**Ukuhlaziya nokuklama**

- **Yini ephuka kuqala?** Ethebhini ethi {{ui:Analysis}}, inkinobho ithola inhlanganisela yamandla elawula, ilunga elibuthakathaka, ukuthi imithwalo ingakhula kangakanani ngaphambi kokuthi ilunga lokuqala lifinyelele umkhawulo walo, kanye nephleyti yokugcina noma ithebhu yokusika lapho amandla okusika kwama-bolt awo esaziwa khona. Idinga izinhlanganiso nekhodi yokuklama kuqala. Ukumelela, i-block shear, amawelda nokuvuleka akukahlolwa. Umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe angacela okufanayo.
- **Okuphilayo.** Khetha {{ui:Live: solve again after each edit}} bese uhlaka luxazululwa futhi emva kokuhlela ngakunye.
- **Ukhonkithi, okwengeziwe.** Ikholomu eyinqaba kuhlaka olungenakunyakaza iklanywa ngomzuzu wayo owandisiwe (ACI 318-19 6.6.4.5). Isikhathi se-seismic singathathwa ekuhlaziyweni ngendlela ka-Rayleigh. Isilabhu esenziwe ngamapuleti sithola insimbi ngalinye ongqimba oyidingayo kumzuzu wawo, kanye nokusika kwendlela eyodwa. Bona [Hlola izingxenye zekhonkithi wenze imithwalo ye-seismic](topic:concrete-and-seismic).
- **Okokuhlolwa ngunjiniyela.** Imiphumela yezisekelo, ekhonkithini kanye ne-Yini ephuka kuqala iyizinsiza zokubuka. Imithwalo yomoya akukakwakhiwa.

**Kuzo zonke izinhlelo**

- **Izilimi.** Usizo lutholakala ngezilimi ezingama-54. Ezingaphandle kwesiNgisi ziyizinhlobo zomshini kuze kufundwe umuntu wendabuko; eziyishumi ziyizintsha (isiLithuania, isiLatvia, isiBosnia, isiMarathi, isiTelugu, isiAlbania, isiAfrikaans, isiFilipino, isiSwahili, isiUzbek).
- **Amakholomu wethuluzi.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Toolbar rows}} ikhetha ukusonga, imigqa emi-2 noma umugqa ow-1. Ibha esivele ilingana ihlala njengoba iyunjalo; eyanele iyaskrola ngecala.
- **Igridi yezakhiwo.** Izinkambu ze-footing zisebenzisa igridi eyabelwanayo: izigaba ezingagoqeka nobuningi obuncane. Ukubala okubhalwe njenge-2*450 kusasebenza.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** Ipadi yethrekhi, ipeni, imbewu ye-3D, isilawuli kanye ne-MIDI kungashayela izinhlelo. Bona [Hamba ku-3D](topic:navigate-3d).
- **Iphaneli ye-Problems.** Izexwayiso zezahlolo ezintsha ezingenhla zivela kwiphaneli efanayo ye-{{ui:Problems}} njengakuqala.
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** Umsizi wakho, oxhunywe nge-CadCraft Studio, usebenza nazo zonke izinhlelo ezisishiyagalombili futhi ubona kuphela lokho okuvumela. Ukuxhuma ngokuchofoza kanye, isixhumi esisesevisini nokhiye we-API wakho ku-Studio kusazofika. Bona [Sebenzisa i-DetailCraft nosizo lwe-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

### Kulungisiwe ku-0.8.1

- **Izilimi ku-Studio.** I-Studio nezinguqulo ezipakishiwe zingashintsha ulimi ngesikhathi sokusebenza; ngaphambili bekungesiNgisi kuphela. Akukahlolwa kuzo zonke izinkundla: sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.
- **Ukukhetha ngewindi elidlula.** Abafakeli be-0.8.1 bahlala bekhetha ngewindi ngendlela efanele njengoba kwakunjalo kwiwebhu ye-0.8; ofakeli omdala abazange.
- **Amathebulethi ngokuma.** Ukuphakama okusele kwegridi kuya ku-canvas, nesakhiwo seshidi siqala ku-760 px noma ngaphansi.
