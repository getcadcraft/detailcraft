Uhlaka lwesampula yimodeli encane ephelele: yonke into i-DetailCraft eyakhayo ivela okungenani kanye. Iyindlela esheshayo yokufunda uhlelo nokuhlola ukuthunyelwa ngaphambi kokuthembela kukho.

## Yivule

1. Khetha {{ui:Sample}} eqenjini le-{{ui:File}}.
2. Uma imodeli yakho yamanje inezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, khetha {{ui:Save first}} noma {{ui:Discard changes}}.
3. Ibha yesimo ithi "Uhlaka lwesampula: amakholomu ayi-6 anamaphuleti esisekelo, amabhimu ayi-4 anamaphuleti okuphela, ibhulaketi elilodwa, izisekelo ezi-7 nezinti zensimbi."

Isampula ivulwa njengemodeli entsha ebizwa ngokuthi "Uhlaka lwesampula". Ayishintshi okunye, futhi ayinalutho lokuhlehlisa.

## Okukuyo

- **Amagridi** 1, 2, 3 ku-6000 mm kanye no-A, B ku-7000 mm. **Amazinga**: Izinga 1 ku-0 no-Izinga 2 ku-4000.
- **Amakholomu ayisithupha**, W12x50, kusuka ku-Izinga 1 kuya ku-Izinga 2 ezindaweni zonke zokuhlangana, ngayinye ephezu kwephuleti lesisekelo elingu-25 mm nezinduku ezine zokubamba.
- **Amabhimu amane**, W16x26, ku-Izinga 2 eduze kwemigqa yegridi A no-B, phezulu kwensimbi ezingeni, nephuleti lokuphela elibhawodiwe elilingene emaphethelweni omabili (ayisishiyagalombili ngakho).
- **Ibhulaketi elilodwa**, HSS4x4x1/4, kusuka ku-A/1 ku-Izinga 1 kuya ku-A/2 ku-Izinga 2.
- **Izisekelo zepedi ezisithupha**, 2000 x 2000 x 600, ngayinye ngaphansi kwekholomu ngalinye, nemethi eyigobe ngaphansi ngezinhlangothi zombili.
- **Esinye isisekelo** eduze kohlaka, 2400 x 1800 x 600, nezinti zokuqala ezisithupha ezinamahhuku ezimile zepiya lekhonkolithi.

## Izinto ongazama

1. Chofoza ibhimu. Funda {{ui:Part mark}}, {{ui:Assembly}}, {{ui:Cut length}} kanye {{ui:Cut back}}: ubude bokusika bufushane kunegridi engu-6000 mm ngoba iphuleti ngalinye lokuphela lisika ibhimu libuyele emuva ngohhafu wobujulile bekholomu kanye nobukhulu bephuleti.
2. Chofoza ikholomu. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Holes}} kukhona izimbobo ezenziwe amaphuleti okuxhuma ku-flange yalo ("8 holes from connections"; amakholomu aphakathi anezingu-16, isethi eyodwa kuwo wonke ama-flange).
3. Ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Part marks}} ngakwesobunxele, bona ukuthi wonke amabhimu amane abelana ngomaki owodwa: ayisinsimbi efanayo. Amakholomu ezingqikithi nawo abelana ngomaki, nakuba izimbobo zawo zibheke ezindleleni ezihlukene, ngoba ikholomu ejikwe ekugcineni nasekuqaleni ingumkhiqizo ofanayo. Amakholomu aphakathi, anezimbobo kuzo zombili ama-flange, athola omunye umaki.
4. Chofoza iphuleti lokuphela (noma enye yamabhawodi ayo). Ipaneli {{ui:Properties}} ibonisa izilungiselelo zephuleti, zebhawodi nokushisela, amabhawodi ayo adalwayo (nobude bawo obubalwe kusuka ekubambeni) kanye nanoma yiziphi izexwayiso zebanga emaphethelweni.
5. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Bill of materials}}. Izinto, ukuhlanganiswa (ibhimu nemaphuleti aphezu kwalo amabili kuyinto eyodwa), namabhawodi nezinduku zokubamba zibalwe nezisindo. Kufanele ubone amamaki ayisithupha: c1 (amakholomu amabili aphakathi) no-c2 (amakholomu ezingqikithi ayi-4), kokubili W12x50 ku-3975 mm; b1, amabhimu ayi-4 W16x26 ku-5650 mm; br1, ibhulaketi; p1, amaphuleti okuphela ayi-8 PL20x180; no-p2, amaphuleti esisekelo ayisithupha PL25x400. Izinto zokuxhuma ngamabhawodi angu-64 20 x 70 A325 nezinduku zokubamba ezingu-24 20 x 575.
6. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Bending schedule}}. Izisekelo ezisithupha ezifanayo zabelana ngamamaki ezintini; isisekelo sepiya sengeza sona.
7. Chofoza ibhulaketi bese ubheka iqembu le-{{ui:Output}}: {{ui:Shop drawing}} ilimele, ngoba imidwebo yokukhiqiza imboza amaphrofayili e-I namaphuleti kuphela kuze kube manje.
8. Khetha {{ui:NC files}}. I-zip iqukethe ifayela lalo lonke amaki ePhrofayili e-I namaphuleti; ibha yesimo ibala amaki ebhulaketi njengangabhalwanga.
9. Khetha {{ui:IFC}} bese uvula ifayela kunoma yisiphi isibuki se-IFC ukuze uhlole ukuthi olunye usofthiwe wakho luyilanda kanjani.
