Le ndikimba ithatha ikholomu eliyisikwele ekungeni kuze kube yincazelo ehloliwe, edwetshiwe futhi ehlelwe. Imithetho ejwayelekile yamabhimu, amaslabhu nezindonga itholakala ku-[Qinisa ikhonkrithi elimise](topic:reinforced-concrete); lena yingxenye yekholomu kuyo, nezinombolo ongazisebenzisa kabusha.

:::warning
I-DetailCraft ichaza izinti onikeza yona. Ayiziklami. Izinti ezilandelayo ziyizinombolo zokuqala zesibonelo, hhayi isincomo: injiniyela eqopha ikhetha usayizi, inani nesikhala.
:::

## Ikholomu lingani lapha

Ikholomu elisikwele linobubanzi (ngokuya kuka-x wendawo yalo) nokujula okungaphesheya (ngokuya kuka-y wendawo yalo), ukumbozwa okucacile kuya ezibopheni, izinti ezinde ngakwesiphambano ngasinye nezibopho ezivaliwe. Ukulijikeleza kujikeleza konke; izihlangothi zisahlala "ubuso bobubanzi" no "ubuso bokujula".

- Izinti ezinde zinikezwa njengenombolo ebusweni bobubanzi nenombolo ebusweni bokujula. Izinti zamakhona zibalwa kokubili, ngakho u-3 no-3 benza izinti ezi-8; u-4 no-4 benza ezi-12.
- Izibopho ziyizikwele ezivaliwe ezinamakhonco amabili ekhoneni elilodwa, 135° noma 90°. Ibopho lokuqala libekwe ngokuphakama okuthile phezu kwephansi (nangaphansi kwelilandelayo), izindawo zokugcina zinesikhala esiseduze, maphakathi kubanzi kakhulu.
- Okwenziwa yizinti phezulu: ziqhubeke bese zixhuma nezinti ezingenhla, zigoqe ngamakhonco ayi-90° zingene ephansi, noma zime ngaphansi kwaphezulu.

## Ikholomu elisebenzisiwe

Ikholomu elingu-400 × 400 mm, i-3600 mm phakathi kwephansi elingaphezulu nephansi elingaphansi, izinti ezinde #8, izibopho #3, ukumbozwa okucacile okungu-40 mm, f'c 28 MPa, fy 420 MPa, ACI 318-19, engaphakathi.

1. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Ukhonkolo}} bese usetha imigqa yegridi namazinga okuma kuwo ikholomu (ipaneli yesobunxele).
2. Ku-{{ui:Engeza ngegridi}}, khetha {{ui:Ikholomu lekhonkrithi}}, khetha ukuhlangana kwegridi namazinga amabili, usethe {{ui:Ububanzi (mm)}} no-{{ui:Ukujula ngobubanzi (mm)}} kube ngu-400 bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza}}.
3. Ephaneli {{ui:Izakhiwo}} setha:
   - {{ui:Ukumbozwa okucacile (mm)}} 40;
   - {{ui:Izinti ezinde}} #8, {{ui:Izinti ohlangothini lobubanzi}} 3, {{ui:Izinti ohlangothini lokujula}} 3;
   - {{ui:Izibopho}} #3, {{ui:Amakhonco}} 135°, {{ui:Isikhala phakathi (mm)}} 300, {{ui:Ubude bendawo yokugcina (mm)}} 600, {{ui:Isikhala endaweni zokugcina (mm)}} 150, {{ui:Okokuqala kusuka ebusweni (mm)}} 50;
   - {{ui:Izinti ezingaphezulu}} {{ui:Qhubeka ubambelele nezinti ezingenhla}}.

Ushintsho ngalunye yisinyathelo esisodwa sokubuyisela emuva.

## Izibopho ezihlangabezanayo

Ngebopho elilodwa lomphetho, izinti zamakhona kuphela ziyabanjwa. I-ACI 318-19 25.7.2.3 ivumela izinti eziphakathi ebusweni ukuthi zingabi nokusekelwa uma zisemgceni ongu-150 mm ngaphakathi kwesinti sekhoneni. Ngezinti ezi-3 ebusweni, njengalapho ngenhla, lokho kuyafezeka. Ngezinti ezi-4 noma ngaphezulu ebusweni akufezeki, futhi i-DetailCraft iyakutshela njengesexwayiso ({{ui:Ukuhlola}} ne-{{ui:Izinkinga}}): "izibopho ezihlangabezanayo" zidingeka. **Izibopho ezihlangabezanayo azikadwetshwa**: zengeze ngesandla emdwebeni, noma khetha izinti ezimbalwa ebusweni.

Ngo-EN 1992-1-1, i-DetailCraft ihlola usayizi wesinti (okungenani 8 mm), inani lezinti (okungenani 4), indawo yensimbi (0.2 % kuya ku-4 % wesigaba), isikhala esicacile nesikhala sezixhumanisi (9.5.2 no-9.5.3).

## Ukuxhuma, isisekelo nekholomu elingaphansi

- Lapho **isisekelo semodeli sima ngaphansi kwekholomu**, ikholomu lixhuma nezinti zokuqala zesisekelo, nokuxhuma kwazo nakho kuyahlolwa.
- Lapho **enye insika yemodeli ima ngaphansi kwayo**, ixhuma nezinti zalelo kholomu.
- Nikeza {{ui:Ubude bokuxhuma (mm, 0 = njengoba kudingeka)}} wakho ukuze uyeqe; okufushane kunokudingekayo kuyinkinga futhi kumakwa * ethebhuleni.
- Ukuxhuma kokucindezela kusetshenziswa ngokuzenzakalelayo ({{ui:Ukuxhuma kokucindezela}} ezilungiselelweni); khetha {{ui:Ukuxhuma kokuhudulwa}} lapho ikholomu lingaba ngaphansi kokuhudulwa.

## Amamaki, uhlelo neshidi lokukhiqiza

Izinti ezifanayo zabelana ngophawu olulodwa: izinti ezinde zekholomu elingenhla ziyi-8C1, izibopho zalo ziyi-17C2 (inombolo kuqala, bese kuba uhlobo lwe-C yekholomu nenombolo elandelanayo). Zivela ethebhini le-{{ui:Uhlelo lokugoba}}, nobude bokusika nesisindo, nakufayela le-CSV elivela ku-{{ui:Okukhiphayo}} › {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Ukugoba}}.

Khetha {{ui:Umdwebo wokukhiqiza}} ngasensikeni ukuze uthole ishidi enekholomu ekubukeni kohlangothi (zonke izinti, amaphawu azo, izindawo zezibopho nokuxhuma ezilinganisiwe) nesigaba esiphakathi, kulandele ishidi lokugoba izinti. Gcina {{ui:Leli shidi njenge-PDF}}.

## Okungakwenzi

- Amakholomu ayindilinga namaspirali, namakholomu angewona amakhona amane: akakakwakhiwa.
- Izibopho zamakhona nezihlangabezanayo ngaphakathi kwesigaba, nokuqinisa okungokokuzamazama (isahluko 18 se-ACI 318-19): akakakwakhiwa.
- Ukuhlanganiswa ezindaweni ozikhethela zona, noma okwehlukanisiwe: zonke izinti zixhuma endaweni eyodwa.
- Ukuthi izinti zanele yini umthwalo: lokho yiklanywa lenjiniyela.
