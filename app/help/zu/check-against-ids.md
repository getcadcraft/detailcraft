## Okuzokwenza

I-IDS (Information Delivery Specification, isilinganiso se-buildingSMART) uhlu lwalokho okufanele kube nakho i-IFC. I-DetailCraft ihlola i-IFC ezoyikhipha (ifayela elifanayo elibhalwa yi-{{ui:IFC}}) ngokuya ku-IDS onikezwe yona, futhi ibhale uhlu lwazo zonke izingxenye ezingafinyelelile.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula noma udwebe imodeli yakho.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Hlola ngokuya ku-IDS…}} eqenjini {{ui:Okukhiphayo}} ethuleni lezisetshenziswa.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Layisha ifayela le-.ids…}} bese ukhetha ifayela. Akukho okulayishwa kuseva.
4. Funda umphumela: isici ngasinye nge-{{ui:Kudlule}} noma {{ui:Kwehlulekile}} nokuthi sisebenza ezingxenyeni ezingaki.
5. Vula Izinkinga ({{key:Ctrl+Shift+M}}) bese uchofoza ukwehluleka: insika, ukuxhumana, isitebhisi noma isisekelo esiphuma kuso siyakhethwa futhi umbono uyasondela kuso.

## Okuzobona

- Isexwayiso sinye ngento ngayinye ekhishiwe nento edingayo, ngegama lesici, okufunwa yi-IDS nokukhishwe.
- Umbiko wokuhlola (ku-Izinkinga) uqala nge-IDS: isihloko sayo, inguqulo, umbhali nezibalo zesici ngasinye.

## Okuwusizo ukukwazi

- I-IDS igcinwa kwifayela lemodeli, ngakho iyahlolwa futhi uma uvula imodeli. Ukulayisha noma ukukhohlwa kwayo kuyisinyathelo esisodwa ongasibuyisela emuva.
- Ushintsho ngalunye luyahlolwa futhi kamuva kancane; kumodeli enkulu ukuhlola kulinda isikhathi esithe xaxa phakathi kokuqhutshwa.
- Ama-GlobalId okukhishwa avela egameni lemodeli nezinombolo zezingxenye ngaphakathi kwe-DetailCraft, ngakho ahlala efanana kusukela kokuhlola kuya kokulandelayo. Ukuqamba kabusha imodeli kunikeza zonke izinto ama-GlobalId amasha.
- Izinombolo ziqhathaniswa ngamayunithi e-SI (ukukhishwa kungamamillimitha; ubude buqala buguqulwe bube ngamamitha).

- Amaphethini ku-IDS afundwa njengexabiso eliphelele. `^` ekuqaleni kanye no `$` ekugcineni nakho kuthathwa njengo "xabiso eliphelele", njengoba iningi lamafayela e-IDS libakusho.
