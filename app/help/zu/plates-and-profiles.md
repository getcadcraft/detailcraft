## Beka ipuleti

Ipuleti libekwa njengebhimu: lihamba phakathi kwezindawo ezimbili zokuhlangana, nobubanzi bungena kuwo nokuqina kuphakama.

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipuleti}} eqenjini {{ui:Beka}}, noma cindezela {{key:P}}.
2. Epaneli {{ui:Izakhiwo}}, setha {{ui:Ukuqina (mm)}} no-{{ui:Ububanzi (mm)}}. Okuzenzekelayo ngu-20 x 200 ku-A36.
3. Setha {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}} no-{{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}} uma kudingeka. Ukujikeleza okungu-90 kumisa ipuleti ngomphetho.
4. Chofoza izindawo ezimbili zokuhlangana, noma sebenzisa ifomu {{ui:Engeza ngegridi}}.

Igama leprofayili yepuleti libhalwa `PL<thickness>x<width>`, isibonelo PL20x200. Ubude bayo ukuhamba phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili.

Ukuze ushintshe usayizi wepuleti elibekiwe, likhethe bese ushintsha {{ui:Ukuqina (mm)}} noma {{ui:Ububanzi (mm)}}.

Amapuleti oyabeka azingxenye zawo (imaka p, ukuhlanganisa P). Ama-end plate nama-base plate akhiwa uxhumano nawo ayamapuleti futhi, kodwa uwashintsha ngoxhumano lwawo; bona [Ama-end plate](topic:end-plates).

## Engeza iphrofayili yezifiso

Lapho isigaba odingayo singekho ekhathalogi, sakhe ngezilinganiso zaso. Iphrofayili yezifiso iyeqembu lemodeli futhi igcinwa kanye nayo. Ukuze uyisebenzise kuzo zonke izimodeli ezintsha, yigcine ku-My profiles, okuchazwe ngaphansi kwe-"Gcina iphrofayili yamamodeli amasha" elingezansi.

1. Ku-{{ui:Imodeli}} ipaneli ngakwesobunxele, vula {{ui:Iphrofayili yezifiso}}.
2. Thayipha {{ui:Igama}}, isibonelo `W8x18-custom` noma `RHS 120x80x5`. Alikwazi ukuba yigama lekhathalogi noma libonakale njengegama lepuleti.
3. Khetha {{ui:Isimo}}: {{ui:I-shape}}, {{ui:Ichaneli}}, {{ui:I-engeli elinganayo}}, {{ui:Ithyubhu elinxande}}, {{ui:Ithyubhu elijikelezayo}} noma {{ui:Ibha elicwebile}}.
4. Gcwalisa izilinganiso ezivela, zonke ngamamilimitha:

| Isimo | Izilinganiso |
|---|---|
| I-shape | Ukujula d, Ububanzi beflenji bf, iwebu tw, iflenji tf, iradiyasi yefillet r (ingaba ngu-0) |
| Ichaneli | Ukujula d, Ububanzi beflenji bf, iwebu tw, iflenji tf (amakhona asikwele) |
| I-engeli elinganayo | Ububanzi b (kuwo wonke umlenze), Ukuqina t |
| Ithyubhu elinxande | Ukuphakama h, Ububanzi b, Ukuqina t (iradiyasi yekhona langaphandle 2t) |
| Ithyubhu elijikelezayo | Idayamitha D, Ukuqina t |
| Ibha eyisicaba | Ububanzi b, Ukuqina t |

5. Shiya {{ui:Gcina ku-My profiles}} ichofoziwe ukuze ibe kuyo yonke imodeli entsha (bona ngezansi), noma ususe ukuchofoza ukuze ihlale kule modeli kuphela.
6. Khetha {{ui:Engeza iphrofayili}}.

Ibha yesimo ithi "Iphrofayili "…" yengeziwe; isemalini yonke yeprofayili ngaphansi kwe-Custom." Ivela ohlwini oluphansi kwefomu nasekugcineni kwazo zonke izinhlu ze-{{ui:Iphrofayili}}.

I-DetailCraft ihlola ukuthi isimo singakhona. Uzobona imilayezo efana nala ethi "Iwebu namaflenji akufanele ngaphakathi kokujula nobubanzi." , "Iradiyasi yefillet inkulu kakhulu kweflenji." noma "Udonga lukhulu kakhulu." Lungisa izinombolo bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza iphrofayili}} futhi.

### Okuthola iphrofayili yezifiso

- Ubunzima ngemitha kusuka endaweni × 7850 kg/m³ (usayizi wekhathalogi usebenzisa ubunzima obushicilelwe).
- Ama-I-shape wezifiso athatha izimbobo, ama-cope, ama-end plate, amafayela e-NC namadrowing eshophu njengama-I-shape wekhathalogi.
- Amabha asicaba wezifiso aziphatha njengamapuleti.
- Amachaneli wezifiso namathyubhu athatha izimbobo; ama-engeli nemithi emijikelezayo awakakwenzi.

Awukwazi ukuhlela noma ukususa iphrofayili yezifiso emodelini ngemva kokuyengeza (sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}} ngokushesha uma wenze iphutha). Nika eyelungisiwe igama elisha.

## Gcina iphrofayili yamamodeli amasha

{{ui:My profiles}}, ngaphansi kwefomu, uhlu lwakho lweprofayili yezifiso. Igcinwa kulesi sishayeli, kulo mshini, hhayi efayeleni lemodeli nhlobo hhayi ku-akhawunti yakho.

- Iphrofayili oyengeza ngenkinobho {{ui:Gcina ku-My profiles}} ichofoziwe iya kuhlu. Yonke imodeli entsha, kuhlanganise nale i-DetailCraft ayivula uma uyiqalisa, iqala nazo zonke ezinhlu zayo zeprofayili.
- Iphrofayili esevele emodelini kodwa engekho kuhlu inenkinobho {{ui:Gcina}} eduze kwayo.
- Kumodeli engenayo enye yeprofayili yakho (ifayela elidala, isibonelo), khetha {{ui:Sebenzisa lapha}} ukuze uyengeze.
- {{ui:Susa}} ikhipha iphrofayili ohlwini. Amamodeli asevele eyisebenzisa agcina ikhophi yawo.
- Imodeli oyivulayo igcina iphrofayili elondolozwe efayeleni layo. Ukuvula ifayela akushintshi uhlu lwakho.
- Igama elisevele kuhlu nezilinganiso ezihlukile liyenqatshwa: nika elisha elinye igama.
- Ukucima idatha yesayithi kwesiphequluli, noma ukusebenzisa esinye isiphequluli noma omunye kompyutha, kuqala ngohlu olungenalutho. Iwindi eliyimfihlo alikwazi ukuyigcina; uhlelo luyakutshela lokho.
