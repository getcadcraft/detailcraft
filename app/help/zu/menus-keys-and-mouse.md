Imiyalo ye-DetailCraft ifinyelelwa ngendlela efanayo noma ngayiphi indlela. Isitayela **semenyu** sinquma kuphela lapho zihlala khona esikrinini, ukuthi zibizwa ngani lapho, nokuthi ngawaphi amakhiye ziyasebenza. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:DetailCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi sisekeli siyakukhumbula.

## Izitayela ezintathu

| Isitayela | Okutholayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:DetailCraft}} | Ithuluzi lengamuva le-DetailCraft ({{ui:File}}, Beka, {{ui:Connect}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:Output}}) nokhiye bohlamvu olulodwa: {{key:V}} khetha, {{key:B}} ibhimu, {{key:C}} insika, {{key:R}} ibhulaketi, {{key:P}} ipuleti, {{key:F}} isisekelo, {{key:S}} isitebhisi. Isiqalo. |
| {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}} | Kubantu abathayipha imiyalo: ribhoni encane ({{ui:Home}}, {{ui:View}}, {{ui:Output}}) nomugqa womyalo. Thayipha BEAM, COLUMN, BRACE, PLATE, FOOTING, STAIR, ENDPLATE, BASEPLATE, SHEARTAB, SPLICE, GUSSET, U (hlehlisa), ZOOM, PLAN, 3D, DRAWINGS, NC noma HELP bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; {{key:Enter}} ngaphandle kwalutho iphinda umyalo wokugcina, {{key:Esc}} iwusula. Qala ukuthayipha noma ngaphi, bese izinhlamvu ziya emugqeni womyalo. Inkinobho ephakathi ishelelisa; ku-3D, {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ephakathi ziyaphendulela. |
| {{ui:Ribhoni yensimbi}} | Amathebhu njengoba izinhlelo zokwenza imininingwane yensimbi zizihlela: {{ui:Ukumodela}} (izitho, ukhonkrithi, izitebhisi), {{ui:Ukuhlela imininingwane}} (izixhumanisi), {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:Imidwebo}} (imidwebo nemibiko), {{ui:Ukukhiqiza}} (amafayela e-NC, IFC) kanye no-{{ui:View}}, nemenyu ye-{{ui:File}} nezinkinobho ezisheshayo ngakwesokudla. Okhiye bohlamvu olulodwa basalile. Inkinobho ephakathi iyashelelisa; ku-3D inkinobho yesobunxele iyaphendulela. |

Zonke izitayela ziqhubeka nokuba nalo {{key:Mod+Z}}, {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+C}}, {{key:Mod+X}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+Shift+V}}, {{key:Mod+D}} no-{{key:Mod+A}}. Amathebhu eribhoni aphinde ahlanganise {{ui:3D}}, {{ui:Ipulani}}, {{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} no-{{ui:Faka esibonweni}} webha yokubuka.

:::tip
Ukhiye osetshenziswa yisakhiwo sikaDetailCraft kodwa isitayela sakho sanika umyalo omunye usungasebenzi, futhi umugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi umyalo wawo uhambe kuphi ("Kwi-Ribhoni yensimbi yami, {{ui:Insika}} ingu-Ctrl+Alt+C"). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} nemicibisholo ihlala ibhekisa okwayo, futhi ngenkathi ingxenye isakhiwa ngesigamu noma ikhophi lilandela isikhombi, izinhlamvu ziba ezakwa-DetailCraft.
:::

## Yenza ngokwakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Okhiye nemenyu}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi losesho liyasiza). Chofoza {{ui:Engeza ukhiye}} bese ucindezela ukhiye, noma okhiye ababili ngokulandelana ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Ngeribhoni, {{ui:Lapho ihlala khona}} ihambisa umyalo uye kwenye ithebhu nephaneli, emenyu ye-{{ui:File}}, noma ku-{{ui:Okhiye kuphela}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Mouse}}, setha ivili, izinkinobho ezishelelisa amapulani nokubukeka kohlangothi, nokuthi inkinobho ngayinye yenzani ku-3D.
5. Yinike igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Save}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe ngaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho ("Ribhoni yensimbi yami"); esakhelwe ngaphakathi asikaze siguquke. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili ubonakala ngombala obomvu, futhi kufanele ulungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Imouse

- **Ivili lemouse**: {{ui:Scroll up zooms in}} (okokuqala) noma {{ui:Scroll up zooms out}}, nokuthi lishesha kangakanani. Ukukhama kwe-trackpad kusondeza njalo ngendlela eminwe yakho enyakaza ngayo.
- **Shelelisa amapulani nokubukeka kohlangothi nge**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokudla, noma zombili. Ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Khetha}} inkinobho yesobunxele njalo idweba iwindi lokukhetha.
- **Hudula ebhalweni le-3D**: {{ui:Okubukwayo ngakunye}} igcina izinkinobho ze-DetailCraft (sobunxele iyaphendulela, ephakathi iyasondeza, sokudla iyashelelisa). Noma setha inkinobho ngayinye ukuthi iphendulele, ishelelise, isondeze noma ingenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ephendulelayo noma eshelelisayo ziguqula kokubili.

## Thatha nawe

{{ui:Thumela}} ilondoloza isitayela esisetshenzisiwe njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa…}} liyengeza, kusuka kwelinye ikhompyutha noma kumngane. Ifayela elingenisiwe lingakhomba kuphela imiyalo ye-DetailCraft: okunye okukulo kushiywe futhi kubalwe emugqeni wesimo. {{ui:Susa lesi sitayela}} isusa eyakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi sisekeli. {{ui:Sebenzisa leli sitayela kuzo zonke izinhlelo}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi esitayela yenza ukuthi zonke izinhlelo zivule esitayelweni esihambelana nazo (ithuluzi lazo, ribhoni noma ukudweba kwakudala), noshintsho oluwenzile kokuthi ngalinye. I-DetailCraft akunayo imenyu-bha yesitayela: lapho imenyu-bha yenye i-app ikhethwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo, i-DetailCraft igcina ithuluzi layo.
:::
