Lo mbhalo uhlobene nezisekelo zephedi elinamakhona. Amabhimu, izinsika, islebhu nezindonga zichazwe ku-[Detail reinforced concrete](topic:reinforced-concrete). Isisekelo ngasinye sithola umata ongaphansi ngezinhlangothi zombili, nama-amabha okuqalisa aphakama aphuma kuwo uma uwafuna. Amabha ahlala ku-US #3 kuya ku-#11.

## Beka isisekelo

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipulani}} bese ukhetha izinga lophahla lwesisekelo.
2. Khetha {{ui:Isisekelo}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Beka}}, noma cindezela {{key:F}}.
3. Setha isisekelo kupaneli ye-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} (bheka ngezansi), noma gcina izilungiselelo ezizenzakalelayo.
4. Chofoza ukuhlangana kwegridi. Isisekelo sibekwa phakathi kwalo, ophahleni lwaso kulelo zinga. Ibha yesimo ithi, isibonelo, "Isisekelo 2000 x 2000 x 600 ngaphansi kwe-A/1."

Ungasebenzisa futhi ifomu le-{{ui:Engeza ngegridi}} phansi kwepaneli.

Isisekelo sidwetshwa sibonakala ngaphakathi ukuze ubone amabha aso, adwetshwe njengamatyubhu aqinile.

## Shintsha isisekelo esibekiwe

Khetha isisekelo (chofoza ukhonkolo waso kude nanoma yiyiphi insika, noma enye yamabha aso). Uthola izinkambu ezifanayo ngaphezu kwalokho {{ui:Isikhungo x}}, {{ui:Isikhungo y}}, {{ui:Phezulu z}} kanye {{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}}. Ngaphansi kwazo kukhona ithebula elibonisa amabha alesi sisekelo ngamamaki, izinombolo, namabubanzi awo, kanye nevolumu kakhonkolo ngamamitha amakhyubhu.

Ushintsho ngalunye luyahlolwa ngaphambi kokugcinwa. Ushintsho olungakhiwa alwenqatshelwa, nebha yesimo ichaza ngani, isibonelo "Ukumbozwa akushiyi indawo yamabha kulesi sisekelo.", "Isisekelo sijulile kakhulu ukuze kube namazendlalelo amabili amabha angaphakathi kokumbozwa." noma "Amabha okuqalisa awafaki ngaphakathi kwesisekelo ngokumbozwa okungu-75 mm."

Ushintsho olungakhiwa kodwa oludinga ukubhekwa luhlwanyelwa ngaphansi kwesisekelo nasethebhuleni {{ui:Izinkinga}}:

- "Amabha ka-#11 ku-59.6 mm ashiya okungaphansi kuka-35.8 mm okucacile (ACI 318-19 25.2.1)." Ibanga elicacile phakathi kwamabha kufanele libe okungenani ububanzi bebha futhi okungenani ku-25.4 mm.
- "Amahuku ka-#5 adinga ujulile ongu-254 mm; ku-150 mm kuphela okufaka ngaphakathi kokumbozwa." Imilenze yamahuku ngakho inqunywa ifushane ukuze ifake, ngakho ayisaba amahuku ajwayelekile.
- "Amahuku okuqalisa afika ngaphakathi kokumbozwa."
- "Amabha okuqalisa aseduze kakhulu kunebanga elincane elicacile."

## Amamaki amabha nohlu lokugoba

Amabha afanayo (usayizi, isimo nezilinganiso kuya ngamamilimitha) ahlanganyela imaki yebha eyodwa kuzo zonke izisekelo. Amamaki abhalwa `<inombolo yebha>F<n>`: 5F1, 5F2 wamabha #5, 6F1 webha lokuqala #6, njalo njalo. Izisekelo ezinosayizi, ukhonkolo, umata namabha okuqalisa ofanayo zabelana ngemaki yesisekelo: F1, F2 …

1. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Uhlelo lokugoba}} ngaphansi. Umugqa ngamunye ungumaki webha munye: usayizi, isimo, izilinganiso A, B no C, inani, ubude bokusika, ubude obuphelele, isisindo ngemitha, isisindo ngasinye, isisindo esiphelele, nokuthi ibha isetshenziselwa ini.
2. Khetha {{ui:Landa i-CSV}} kuleli thebhu, noma {{ui:Ukugoba}} kumenyu ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} yeqembu {{ui:Okukhiphayo}}, ukuthola `<name> bending schedule.csv`.

Izimo ziwukhodi zalo i-DetailCraft, hhayi ezesitendi esithile: S kuqondile, L igoba elilodwa, U amagoba amabili. Izilinganiso zisuka ngaphandle kuya ngaphandle; ubude bokusika kuyindlela eqondile ephakathi kwamagoba ngokuhamba kwawo. Bona [Rebar reference](topic:rebar-reference).

## Okungahlolwa yi-DetailCraft

- Ubude bokuthuthuka nokuhlanganisa, nokuthi amabha okuqalisa ade yini ukwanela insika ephezulu.
- Ingxenye yomthetho webanga elicacile ngosayizi wesihlanganisi (ayiwazi umxube).
- Noma yini mayelana nensika nepuleti lesisekelo phezulu kwayo: izisekelo nensimbi zihlukene.
