I-DetailCraft ibhala ifayela elilodwa le-DSTV NC (`.nc1`) ngakunye kokumaka kwengxenye, ngama-I-shape, amachaneli, ama-engeli, i-HSS engunxande nejikelezayo, namapuleti, bese iwahlanganisa ibe yizipakisho. Imigqa yamabhimu namamshini wamapuleti afunda la mafayela ukuze asike, abhoboze nokusike.

## Ukukhipha

1. Sula ithebhu ethi {{ui:Izinkinga}} kuqala: uxhumano olungakwazanga ukwakhiwa alungezi izimbobo.
2. Londoloza imodeli ({{key:Mod+S}}), ukuze amafayela e-NC ahambisane nemodeli elondoloziwe.
3. Khetha {{ui:Amafayela e-NC}} eqenjini {{ui:Okukhiphayo}}.
4. Isiphequluli sidawuniloda i-`<name> NC.zip`. Ibha yesimo ithi kukhona amafayela angaki, futhi ibala noma yimaphi amamaki angazange awabhale (i-engeli noma ithyubhu elijikelezayo elinecope).

Ngaphakathi kwe-zip, ifayela ngalinye lithiwa ngokumaka kwengxenye: `b1.nc1`, `c1.nc1`, `p1.nc1` … I-zip ayicindezelwanga futhi ivulwa yithuluzi lanoma iyiphi i-zip.

Uma imodeli ingenazo izingxenye, ibha yesimo iyasho lokho.

## Okuqukethwe yifayela ngalinye

- **Ikhanda (ST):** igama lemodeli njengomyalo, ukumaka kwengxenye njengedrowing nengxenye, isigaba 1 noma isigaba sengxenye, ikhwalithi yensimbi (igredi yempahla), inani, iphrofayili nezilinganiso zayo, ubunzima ngemitha nendawo yopende ngemitha. Ukusika ngesaha kuyisikwele.
- **Amakhonco angaphandle (AK):** umugqa wewebu namanotshi wama-cope, nemigqa yeflenji efushanisiwe lapho i-cope ilisusa khona.
- **Izimbobo (BO):** yonke imbobo ngohlangothi lwayo, indawo, i-y nobubanzi, kufaka phakathi izimbobo ezenziwe ama-end plate namapuleti esisekelo.

Ngakunye ngokunakekela izinkambu nemithetho yezindawo nezikhundla: [Izinkambu zefayela le-NC](topic:nc1-fields).

## Okungabhalwa

- Ama-engeli nemithi emijikelezayo enama-cope: akukho fayela le-NC. Amamaki abo ahlinzeka ebhalweni lesimo.
- Izimbobo emithini emijikelezayo (i-DetailCraft ayenzi lutho).
- Ama-bevel, amakhonco angaphakathi, izimpawu zokumaka ingxenye (scribing) namakhona wesaha angaphandle kwesikwele.
- Izimbobo ezinde (i-DetailCraft inezimbobo ezijikelezayo kuphela).

## Hlola ngaphambi kokukhiqiza

Amafayela e-NC e-DetailCraft ahlolwa yisuthi yawo yezivivinyo, kodwa awakaqhutshwa emishinini yesikhungo sakho.

1. Vula amafayela ku-NC viewer yesikhungo sakho noma kusofthiwe yomshini, bese uwaqhathanisa namadrowing wokukhiqiza.
2. Hlola ingxenye eyodwa yalolwhlobo ngalunye emshinini ngaphambi kokuqhuba iqoqo.
3. Hlola ukuthi kusofthiwe yakho ifunda kanjani ama-y eflenji nobuso bamachaneli: i-DetailCraft ilinganisa i-y yeflenji kusuka emphethelweni wangemuva, futhi ibeka amachaneli emhlane njengoba isendlalelo siwadweba (bona [Izinkambu zefayela le-NC](topic:nc1-fields#faces-and-coordinates)).
