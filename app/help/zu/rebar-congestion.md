Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Ukuminyana}} idweba imephu eshisayo yesisekelo ngasinye njengoba ibukeka ngaphezulu. I-Pale blue ivulekile; okuphuzi nokuthwesi kuqala ukuqina; okubomvu kuqinile kakhulu kunokudingeka kwezinti. Amaseli amnyama yizinti ngokwazo.

## Ikalwa yimephu

Ukhonkrithi wehla ngaphakathi kwensimbi ngezendlalelo ngezendlalelo futhi angahamba ngokuhlukile phakathi kwezendlalelo. Ngakho ndawonye lapho ithatha ibanga elincane kakhulu elikhululekile kunoma iyiphi endlalelo: phakathi kwezinti zomata wangezansi, phakathi kwendlalelo elandelayo yezinti, nasezinhlanganweni zamahhuku ezinti zokuqala phezulu. Izinti zokuqala zimi kuzo zonke.

## Okubalwa njengokuqinile

Okudingekayo yiyona ekhulu phakathi kwe-25 mm nobubanzi bezinti (ACI 318-19 25.2.1), noma, zezinti ze-BS 4449, ekhulu phakathi kobubanzi bezinti nama-20 mm (EN 1992-1-1 8.2). Amabhokisi amabili aphakamisa lokho:

- {{ui:Izinhlayiya ezinkulu kakhulu}}: okudingekayo kuba ngu-4/3 wobukhulu benhlayiya (ACI) noma inhlayiya kanye ne-5 mm (EN).
- {{ui:Ikhanda le-vibrator}}: okudingekayo kuba okungenani ububanzi bekhanda.

Zombili ziqala ku-0, okusho okungaziwa: i-DetailCraft ayazi ihlanganiso lakho noma ipompo lakho.

## Izinkinga

Isisekelo esinendawo ebanzi kunokudingekayo sifakwa kuhlu lwepaneli {{ui:Izinkinga}} ngendawo yembobo etholakele nembobo edingekayo. {{ui:Khetha}} eduze kwemephu ikhetha isisekelo.

Imephu ayiboni izibopho, izixhumanisi, imijikelezo noma izinti zensika ngokwayo.
