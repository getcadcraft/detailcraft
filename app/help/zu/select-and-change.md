Okunye nokunye kulezi kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kanye kubuyisela konke.

## Khetha ngewindi

- Ku-{{ui:Plan}} ne-{{ui:Elevation}}, donsa ngenkinobho yesobunxele kusuka endaweni engenalutho. Ku-{{ui:3D}}, bamba {{key:Shift}} bese udonsa (ukudonsa okuvamile kujikeleza imodeli).
- Donsa **ngakwesokudla** ukuze uthole iwindi: lithatha okuphelele ngaphakathi kwalo, luboniswe ngomphetho oqinile.
- Donsa **ngakwesobunxele** ukuze uthole iwindi elihlanganayo: lithatha konke elithinta, luboniswe ngemigqa edashiwe.
- Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngesikhathi uqala ukudonsa ukuze wengeze ekukhetheni esikhundleni sokuthi kushintshe.
- Ipulani nelevashini zinikeza kuphela okukhombisayo: izingxenye ezisikwe ngaphezu kwepulani, noma ezingaphezu kwe-1 m kusuka emgqeni wegridi welevashini, azikhethwa.
- {{key:Esc}} ngesikhathi udonsa ulahla iwindi.

### Lasso

Khetha **Lasso** ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Dragging to select}} ku-[izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings) bese udonsa okufanayo kudweba umugqa ongakhethiwe esikhundleni sendandatho.

- Ukunyakaza kokuqala okuya eceleni kuyanquma ukuthi ikhetha kanjani: **ngakwesokudla**, lokho okuphelele ngaphakathi (umugqa oqinile); **ngakwesobunxele**, lokho okuthintayo (okudashiwe).
- Cindezela {{key:Space}} ngesikhathi udonsa ukuze uguqule phakathi kwangaphakathi, kuthintayo nothango: umugqa ovulekile othatha noma yini oyidlulayo (udotshiwe, uwolintshi).
- {{key:Shift}} ukwengeza no-{{key:Esc}} ukulahla kusebenza njengakwewindi.

:::touch
Esikrinini sokuthinta, umunwe owodwa ushelelisa ipulani nelevashini njengakuqala; khetha izingxenye ngokuzithinta.
:::

Ku-{{ui:Plan}} ne-{{ui:Elevation}}, shelelisa ngenkinobho yesokudla noma ephakathi ngenkathi ithuluzi {{ui:Select}} lisebenza.

## Donsa izingxenye ukuze uzihambise

1. Khetha izingxenye kuqala (chofoza, {{key:Shift}}+chofoza noma iwindi).
2. Cindezela ingxenye ekhethiwe bese udonsa. Yonke ikhethwa iyahamba; umugqa ophukile ukhombisa lapho iya khona.
3. Yekela ukuze ubeke. {{key:Esc}} ngaphambi kokuyeka ibuyisela konke endaweni yakho.

- Ku-{{ui:3D}} ne-{{ui:Plan}} izingxenye ziyahamba phakathi kwelevashi. Bamba {{key:Mod}} ngesikhathi uqala ukudonsa ku-{{ui:3D}} ukuze uzihambise phezulu nphansi esikhundleni salokho.
- Ku-{{ui:Elevation}} ziyahamba ngaphakathi kwe-elevashini: ngomgqa wegridi naphezulu nphansi.
- Ingxenye oyibambe iyabamba kuhlangano wegridi uma isiphetho sayo sisondela ngamapikseli ambalwa kuwo (ukuhlangana kuyakhanya nebha yesimo iyiqamba). Kwenye indawo ukuhamba kwenzeka ngezinyathelo ze-10 mm.
- Ukucindezela ingxenye ongayikhethanga kuqhubeka kujikeleze noma kushelelise umbono, ukudonsa akuhambisi lutho ngephutha.
- Ukudonsa kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, njengo-{{ui:Move}}. Ukuxhumana kulandela izinto zakho.

## Shintsha impahla kwezinto eziningi

Khetha izinto ezimbili noma ngaphezulu. Ngaphansi kwe-**Shintsha kuzo zonke** ephaneli {{ui:Properties}}:

- {{ui:Profile}}, {{ui:Position}}: zamabhimu, zezinsika nezinqinisi phakathi kwazo.
- {{ui:Plate thickness (mm)}} no-{{ui:Plate width (mm)}}: zamaphuleti.
- {{ui:Material}} no-{{ui:Rotation (°)}}: zazo zonke.

Inkambu ethi **Ayehluka** ibonisa uma izinto zingavumelani. Khetha noma bhala inani futhi yonke into okusebenza kuyo iyawuthatha; ibha yesimo ithi ukuthi zingaki ezishintshile nokuthi zingaki ezigcine izinto zazo.

Izinkambu zamanani ephaneli {{ui:Properties}} zamukela nokubala: `2*450`, `4500/3`, `(1200-2*25)/2`, `6-1/2`. Ukubala okungafundeki kuyenqatshwa, futhi ibha yesimo ithi iphutha liphi.

## Hambisa, jikeleza noma bonisa

Khetha izinto nezisekelo (eyodwa nayo iyasebenza), bese usebenzisa **Hambisa, jikeleza, bonisa** ephaneli {{ui:Properties}}. Ukuxhumana akuhanjiswa wedwa: kulandela izinto zakho. Uma uhambisa ibhimu kodwa hhayi insika yalo, ibha yesimo ithi zingaki izixhumanisi ezihlanganisa into ehanjisiwe nelehlala lapho, futhi [Izinkinga](topic:troubleshooting) ithi uma zingasafaneleki.

- **Hambisa:** bhala x, y no-z ngamamilimitha bese ukhetha {{ui:Move}}. Noma khetha {{ui:Pick two points}} (noma cindezela {{key:M}}): chofoza ukuhlangana kwegridi okuhanjiswa kuyo, bese okuya kuyo. Umugqa ophukile ulandela isikhombi.
- **Jikeleza epulanini:** bhala i-engle (ngokuphambene nowotshe, ubuka phezulu) bese ukhetha okuzojikeleza ngakho: phakathi kwakho okukhethiwe, noma ukuhlangana kwegridi. Izinsika ziyajikeleza nefreyimu.
- **Bonisa:** khetha umugqa wegridi. Isithombe esibonisiwe yiphrofayili efanayo ejikelezwe ngendlela ehlukile: i-I-shape igcina amaflenji ayo, i-channel ibheka enye indlela (phezulu nphansi kuyashintshana), nezimbobo nezikhefu zihamba nengxenye.
- Hlola **Gcina okokuqala (yenza ikhophi)** ukuze ushiye okukhethiwe lapho kunjalo bese uhambisa, ujikeleze noma ubonise ikhophi. Ukuxhumana kwayo kuyalandela uma {{ui:Copy end plates and base plates with their members}} isebenza ku-[izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings).

Ukujikeleza kwenzeka emgqeni othe xaxa, ukubonisa kusendizeni eqondile, ngakho amazinga ahlala eleveli nezisekelo zihlala zimile.
