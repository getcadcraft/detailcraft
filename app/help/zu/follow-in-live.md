Ku-Live TeamWork session, i-DetailCraft idweba isikhombi somuntu ngamunye emodelini negama lakhe nombala wakhe, bese ibeka ibhokisi ozungeze okukhethwe yiye. Uhlu lwabantu lukuvumela ukulandela omunye wabo.

## Okuzokwenza

Hambisa ukubuka kwakho ndawonye nokwomunye umuntu, futhi, njengomphathi, cela wonke umuntu ukuthi akulandele kwakho. Ukuqala noma ukujoyina isikhathi kwenziwa ku-CadCraft Studio; bheka [Hlela isakhiwo sinye ndawonye nge-Live TeamWork](topic:work-live).

## Izinyathelo

1. Ku-session, khetha {{ui:Abantu}}. Uhlu {{ui:Abantu kulesi sikhathi}} lubonisa ithuluzi ngalinye, ishidi nokuhlelwa kokugcina.
2. Khetha {{ui:Landela}} eduze kwegama. Ukubuka kwakho kuhamba nokwakhe, nebha iyakutshela ukuthi uyamlandela.
3. Khetha {{ui:Yeka ukulandela}} (noma {{ui:Misa}} kubha) ukuze ubuyele embonweni wakho.
4. Njengomphathi, khetha {{ui:Cela wonke umuntu andilandele}}. Wonke umuntu uyacelwa; khetha {{ui:Yeka ukucela wonke umuntu ukuthi andilandele}} ukuze ukuphelise.
5. Vala uhlu ngokukhetha {{ui:Vala}}.

## Amanothi

- Uma umuntu omlandelayo esebhalweni elingalandelwa, uyatshelwa, futhi ukubuka kwakho kuhlala njengoba kunjalo.
- Ukulandela kushintsha okubonakalayo kuphela, hhayi imodeli.
