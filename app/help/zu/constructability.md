I-DetailCraft ibheka izinhlobo ezine zenkinga ezingatholakali ukuhlolwa kwamandla, futhi zibhala zona ephaneli {{ui:Izinkinga}}. Lezo yizexwayiso nezimpawu zokubheka, hhayi amaphutha: imodeli isakhiwa.

## Ibhawodi isipanela esingalifinyelela

Ngebhawodi ngalinye, i-DetailCraft ibeka isokhethi phezu kwekhanda nophethe. Isokhethi lisilinda elibanzi njengophethe ngamakhona awo kanye nodonga lwe-4 mm, elide ngu-60 mm. Uma i-web yebhimu, i-flange, iphuleti noma ibhulaketi lime ngaphakathi kwalesi silinda, inkinga iqamba uxhumo, amabhawodi amaningi kangakanani, ingxenye edlula endleleni nokuthi ikude kangakanani nebhawodi.

Isokhethi libe yisokhethi lomthelela ojwayelekile. Isipanela sidinga indawo encane, nethuluzi elikhulu lidinga enganingi. Cela umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe ukuthi asebenzise `constructability_check` nge-`socketLength` ehlukile ukuze ubone impendulo ishintsha kanjani.

## Amabhawodi aseduze kakhulu kwesokhethi

Isokhethi phezu kwebhawodi elilodwa akufanele lithinte ikhanda lelilandelayo. Amabhawodi exhumanweni aseduze kunobubanzi besokhethi kanye nekhanda lomakhelwane ayabalwa, kube nesikhala esitholakele nesikhala esidingekayo.

## Usayizi wokushisela ngegumbi

I-AISC 360-16 Table J2.4 imisa ukushisela ngegumbi okuncane kakhulu ngokuya ngokuqina kwengxenye encane (3 mm kuya ku-6.35 mm ukuqina, 5 mm kuya ku-12.7, 6 mm kuya ku-19.05, 8 mm ngaphezulu), no-J2.2b okukhulu kakhulu ngaphansi komphetho wempahla: akukhulu kunempahla uma ingaba ngaphansi kwe-6.35 mm, kungenjalo kube ngu-1.6 mm ngaphansi. Uxhumo olunosayizi ofanayo ngokuqina okufanayo ludwetshwa njengenkinga eyodwa.

Ukuqina okusetshenzisiwe kuyingxenye encane kakhulu echazwe ukushisela, ngakho ukushisela okungu-8 mm okuzungeze lonke ibhimu eline-web engu-6.35 mm kuyadwetshwa.

## Amanzi nezimbobo zokuphefumula

- **Ukuphela okuvuliwe kwasezulwini** kwesigaba esiyingiza ngaphakathi esimi ngqo noma esithambekele ngaphezu kuka-10° kuvumela imvula ukuthi ingene.
- Isigaba esingenalutho **esivaliwe emaphethelweni omabili** sidinga imbobo yokuphefumula nembobo yokukhipha; ukugalvanayisa ngokufakwa emanzini ashisayo nengcindezi yomoya oshisayo kokubili kuyadinga omunye.

## Ingabe umshiseli ukwazi ukuyifinyelela?

Ukushisela ngegumbi kwenziwa netoshi phakathi kwamabili emibili ayihlanganisayo. Ngokushisela ngegumbi okuzungeze konke (ipuleti lokuphela, iphuleti lesisekelo, ipuleti lesikhwama) ukuhlola kuthatha isifinyezo sesigaba selunga kwiphuleti futhi, njalo ngama-20 mm, kuhamba indlela yetoshi iphume ngama-45° kusukela lapho inozzle yayo (i-radius engu-10 mm ekuqaleni) isuka khona ibhodla kokubili ubuso, ngama-30 mm amanye. Uma indlela ihlangana nebhimu lalo ngqo noma nenye ingxenye, leyo ngxenye yokushisela ibalwa ngengxenye nengxenye nendawo. Lezi zinombolo zijwayelekile, hhayi izinga lomgomo: buza umenzi wakho ngenqubo kanye nenozzle. Ukushisela ezinhlangothini ezimbili (ithebhu lokusika) akutholakali futhi akuhlolwa.
