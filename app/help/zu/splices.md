Ukuhlanganisa okubotshiwe kuhlanganisa izingxenye ezimbili zesakhiwo esisodwa se-I-shape ngokuphelelana: ipuleti lokumboza ngaphandle kwalo lonke iflenji, nepuleti ngakolunye nakolunye uhlangothi lowebhu, konke kubotshwe ngaphuma kuzo zombili izingxenye. DetailCraft isika izingxenye zibuyele emuva ekuhlukaniseni, ibeka amapuleti amane, ibhoboza izimbobo kumapuleti nakuzo zombili izingxenye, yengeze izikulufo futhi ixwayise ngamabanga emiphetho, ukuma kanye nokuhlelwa.

## Lapho isebenza khona

- Izibhimu ezimbili, noma amalifithi amabili ensika, zesakhiwo se-**I-shape esifanayo**, ezihlangana **ngokuphelelana ngomugqa oyodwa**: imiphetho ingahlukaniswa ngaphezu kwe-50 mm, izingxenye zisebenta futhi zisemgqeni oyodwa (ngaphakathi kwe-1 mm). Ingxenye ngayinye ingadwetshwa noma ngasiphi isiqondiso.
- Izingxenye ezilingene, ezithambekile noma ezimile zonke ziyasebenza.
- Izingxenye ezimbili ezinobujulile noma ububanzi beflenji obuhlukile ziyenqatshwa, kanjalo namachaneli, ama-angle namathubhu.

## Engeza ukuhlanganisa

1. Dweba izingxenye ezimbili ukuze zihlangane ngokuphelelana. Ukuze uhlukanise isakhiwo esesele sidwetshiwe, sidwebe futhi sibe izingxenye ezimbili.
2. Khetha okukodwa (noma womabili) bese ukhetha {{ui:Ukuhlanganisa}} kumenyu {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} yeqembu {{ui:Xhuma}}.
3. Lo nalo lonke uxhumo olukhululekile lwezingxenye ezikhethiwe nengxenye enayo iphrofayili efanayo lithola ukuhlanganisa. Ibha yesimo iyatshela ukuthi zingaki.

Khetha ukuhlanganisa (chofoza okunye kwamapuleti aso) ukuze ushintshe izilungiselelo zakho ephaneli esekhohlo. {{ui:Sebenzisa kokufanayo (n)}} kopisha kuzo zonke ezinye izixhumi ezikhululekile phakathi kwezingxenye zephrofayili efanayo.

| Ububanzi besikulufo, imbobo, igredi, ukuvumela ubude | 20, 22, A325, 30 | Njengakumapuleti okuphela |
| Ukuqina kwepuleti leflenji | 12 | Ipuleti libanzi njengeflenji |
| Izingqimba zezikulufo zeflenji (ohlangothini ngalunye) | 3 | Izingqimba ezinqamula iflenji ohlangothini ngalunye loxhumo |

Ubude bamapuleti kulandela izikulufo: ipuleti leflenji = igebe + 2 × (2 × ibanga lomphetho + (izingqimba − 1) × ukuhamba); amapuleti kawebhu alandela ngokufanayo namakholomu awo. Ngokuzenzakalelayo amapuleti eflenji anobude be-470.

## Okufanele wazi

- Amapuleti ayeka **ingxenye yokuqala edwetshwe** kwezimbili (izakhiwo zayo zibiza ukuthi ingxenye yokuqala): ayabonakala emdwebeni wayo wenhlanganisela futhi ahamba nayo, abotshiwe, kungabi ushukela. Umdwebo wenhlanganisela yengxenye yesibili ubonisa izimbobo zayo kuphela.
- Izikulufo zeflenji zibamba iflenji nepuleti layo; izikulufo zowebhu zibamba uwebhu namapuleti wowebhu womabili. Ubude bezikulufo buhlukile ezakhiweni.
- Izexwayiso zisebenzisa okuncane kwe-EN 1993-1-8 okufanayo nakwezinye izixhumi (ibanga lomphetho nelemiphetho 1.2 × imbobo, ukuhamba 2.2 × imbobo ubude nethe 2.4 × imbobo ububanzi), kanjalo nepuleti lowebhu elijulile kunowebhu ovulekile nezikulufo zeflenji eziseduze kakhulu nowebhu ngokwe-fillet.
- DetailCraft ayiklami ukuhlanganisa (akukho ukuhlola amandla), futhi ayenzi amapuleti okugcwalisa ezingxenyeni ezinobukhulu obuhlukile.
- Ukukopisha zombili izingxenye kukopisha ukuhlanganisa kwazo; ukususa noma iyiphi ingxenye kususa nokuhlanganisa.
