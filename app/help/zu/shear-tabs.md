Ithebhu yokusika (ipuleti elilodwa, ipuleti lesiphiko) iyipuleti eshiselwe ekholomini ngesikhathi sokukhiqiza; endaweni yokwakha iwebhu yebhimu ibhoshelwa kuyo ngomugqa oqondile wamabhawodi. I-DetailCraft ibeka ithebhu, isika ibhimu libuyele emuva lisuka ekholomini, ibhola izimbobo kuthebhu nakuwebhu yebhimu, yengeze amabhawodi nokushisela, futhi ixwayise ngamabanga emaphethelweni nokulingana.

## Lapho isebenza

- Ibhimu (iphrofayili ye-I noma ichaneli) elinesiphetho salo emgqeni we-I-shape ekholomini **eqondile**, lihlangana **nesiphiko** noma **newebhu** yalo.
- **Eqondile, eyingalingani epulanini, noma ethambekile**: ithebhu ilandela iwebhu yebhimu, bese ibhimu lisikwa libuyele emuva ukuze ikhoni yalo eseduze igcine igebe ekholomini. Ibhimu elikhuphuka noma elehla ngaphezu kuka-60° liyenqatshwa.

## Engeza amathebhu okusika

1. Khetha ibhimu.
2. Eqenjini le-{{ui:Connect}}, vula {{ui:More}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Shear tabs}} (noma khetha {{ui:Add shear tabs}} ephaneli {{ui:Properties}}).
3. Yonke imbobo yebhimu ehlangana nekholomu ithola ithebhu. Isiphetho sebhimu ngasinye sithatha uxhumo olulodwa: ithebhu yokusika noma iphuleti lokuphela.

Khetha ithebhu (chofoza kuyo, noma ibhawodi) ukubona nokushintsha izilungiselelo zayo. {{ui:Apply to similar}} iyikopisha njengethebhu yokusika kuso sonke isixhumo esifanayo esingenalo uxhumo.

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- Ithebhu iyingxenye yokuhlanganiswa kwekholomu (eshiselwe ku-shop); ukuhlanganiswa kwebhimu kunezimbobo kuphela.
- Ibhimu elingalingani noma elithambekile lisikwa lingene emuva kakhulu kunelieqondile ukuze ikhoni yalo eseduze igcine igebe. I-DetailCraft isika amabhimu aqondile: umkhiqizi usika isiphetho ngokuvumelana nakho. Umphetho wethebhu ekholomini nawo udwetshwe eqondile.
- I-DetailCraft ayihloli amandla. Izexwayiso zisebenzisa amanani ancane e-EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.3 wamabanga emaphethelweni nokubekwa, futhi ziyasho uma ithebhu ijule kakhulu kunewebhu yebhimu phakathi kwama-fillet wayo, noma amabhawodi emi eseduze kakhulu nesiphetho sebhimu esisikiwe.
- [Ukuhlola ukungqubuzana](topic:clash-check) kushiya ithebhu nezinto ezixhumanisayo ngaphandle: zihloselwe ukuhlangana.
