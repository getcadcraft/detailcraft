Lokhu yiyini eshintshile ku-DetailCraft 0.8 (1 Novemba 2026) nasku-0.8.1 (2 Novemba 2026), uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.7. I-0.7 yahlola izinto zensimbi; i-0.8 ihlela ukuphakanyiswa, iphakamisa uxhumano, ihlola izisekelo nerebar, futhi yakha izindonga, izindawo zokuhamba nophahla lwezinkuni nezinsimbi ezincane.

## Okusha

### Ukumisa

- **Uhlelo lokumisa nefilimu.** Ethebhini ethi {{ui:Erection}}, chaza ikhreyini (lapho imi khona, ihuku layo eliphezulu kakhulu, nokubopha okungekho eshadini), bese uthayipha imigqa yeshadi layo lomthwalo. I-DetailCraft iphakamisa imodeli inhlanganisela eyodwa ngesikhathi, ngokulandelana kwesigaba nezinga ngezinga, futhi ihlola ngalinye lokuphakamisa ngeshadi endaweni yalo nangomkhawulo owusethile. I-{{ui:Save movie}} ibhala ifayela elilodwa le-HTML elidlala ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kunoma yisiphequluli, nakufoni. Ukuphakamisa okungaphezu kweshadi, okudlula kulo noma okuphakeme kakhulu kuthi {{ui:Problems}}. Kuwuhlelo olusuka ezinombolweni zakho, hhayi uhlelo lokuphakamisa olusemthethweni. Bona [Uhlelo lokumisa nefilimu](topic:erection-movie).

### Uxhumano

- **I-autopilot yokuxhumana.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Autopilot}} ibheka yonke indawo yokuhlangana engenaxhumano bese iphakamisa enye ngokwezimo: amaphuleti esisekelo, amathebhu okusika, amaphuleti okuphela wamabhimu owamaka njengamabhimu wesikhathi, ukuhlanganisa nezinsimbi zokuqinisa. Nika umthwalo ofanayo noma ukusika kwesinye sokuphela bese inani lezikulufo lilandela amandla okuklama esikulufo. I-{{ui:Add them all}} yengeza iziphakamiso njengesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ziyizinani zokuqala ukuze ubuyekeze, hhayi ukuklama koxhumano.
- **Ukuhlolwa kokwakheka.** {{ui:Problems}} ngesikulufo esingafinyelelwa yisipanela, izikulufo ezisondelene kakhulu kwisokhethi, ukushisela kwamaphrofayili kakhona okungaphezu kwemikhawulo yosayizi wakho, nezigaba ezigobile ezibamba amanzi. Ukufinyelela kokushisela kusha: ukushisela kwamaphrofayili okuzungeze konke engxenyeni engafinyeleleki yithochi yomshisi listwa ngengxenye edlula engxenyeni elimele nengxenye yokushisela ethintekayo. Bona [Ukuhlolwa kokwakheka](topic:constructability).

### Izisekelo ne-rebar

- **Ukuhlolwa kwezisekelo.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Foundation}} ihlola izisekelo, izisekelo ezihlanganisiwe nama-mat endaweni yomhlaba oyichazile: ukuthwala, ukudonsela nokuguquka kusuka emithwalweni yokusebenza; ukugoba, ukusika, ukuphunga, ukuthuthukiswa kanye nensimbi encane kakhulu kusuka emithwalweni eyenziwe (ACI 318-19). Ikholomu eseduze nomphetho noma ikhona ihlolwa ngesigaba esisikwe ngumphetho wesisekelo. Ukuhla kuvela kwimodulasi yomhlabathi, i-mat ethambile ixazululwa phezu kwezipringi ngemodulasi yendawo ephansi, namaqembu amaphayili nezindonga zokubamba zinamaphaneli azo. Imithwalo yekholomu ingathayipha noma ithathwe ku-{{ui:Analysis}}. Bona [Ukuhlolwa kwezisekelo](topic:foundation-checks).
- **Imephu yokuminyana kwerebar.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Congestion}} idweba imephu eshisayo yesisekelo ngasinye njengoba ibonakala kusuka phezulu: lapho izinti zishiya indawo encane kakhulu ukuthi ukhonkolo ufinyelele phansi, ngokumelene nomthetho wesikhala esicacile nangokuba uwunikeze, ukuhlanganiswa okukhulu kakhulu nekhanda lesihlukanisi. Bona [Imephu yokuminyana kwerebar](topic:rebar-congestion).

### Ukwakha nokuhlinzeka

- **Ukwakha ngokhuni nangensimbi enesisindo esincane.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Framing}} yakha amaphaneli wezindonga, wezindawo zokuhamba nophahla ngayo yonke ingxenye, uhlu lokusika, isitoko okufanele usithenge nomdwebo wokukhiqiza ngesilinganiso. I-{{ui:Place as members}} ibeka izingxenye emodelini, ne-{{ui:PDF sheets}} ilondoloza ishidi elilodwa ngephaneli ngalinye. Osayizi, izikhala nezinsika ngabakho: i-DetailCraft ayihloli ukuthi zithwala umthwalo. Bona [Amaphaneli wokwakha ngokhuni nangensimbi enesisindo esincane](topic:framing-panels).
- **Isitoko somhlinzeki nezikhathi zokulinda.** Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Supply}} ifunda uhlu lwesitoko somhlinzeki (CSV) ibonise, ngayinye yezigaba uhlu lokusika olizithengayo, ukuthi kukhona yini okwanele esitokweni nokuthi ukulinda kuzothatha isikhathi esingakanani. Okufushane nezigaba ezilibele sikhona ku-{{ui:Problems}}. Akukho ukuyalwa okwenziwayo. Bona [Isitoko somhlinzeki nezikhathi zokulinda](topic:supplier-stock).

### Ukuhlaziya nokuklama

- **Yini ephuka kuqala?** Ethebhini ethi {{ui:Analysis}}, inkinobho ithola inhlanganisela yamandla elawula, ilunga elibuthakathaka kakhulu, umthwalo ungakhula kangakanani ngaphambi kokuthi ilunga lokuqala lifinyelele umkhawulo waloo, nesixhumanisi esibuthakathaka kakhulu noma ithebhu yokusika lapho amandla okusika kwezikulufo zalo esaziwa. Idinga izinhlanganisela nekhodi yokuklama kuqala. Ukuthwala, ibhulokhi yokusika, izinsimbi zokushisela nokusika akuhlolwa. Umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe angacela okufanayo.
- **Okuphilayo.** Khetha {{ui:Live: solve again after each edit}} bese uhlaka luxazululwa futhi emva kokuhlela ngakunye.
- **Ukhonkolo, okwengeziwe.** Ikholomu emincane kuhlaka olungaphezu kokuzinzile ikhonkolo idizaina ngenxa yomzuzu wayo oluphindwe kabili (ACI 318-19 6.6.4.5). Isikhathi se-seismic singathathwa ku-analysis ngendlela ka-Rayleigh. Isilabhu esenziwe ngamaphuleti sithola insimbi isigaba ngasinye sidinga kumaminithi wayo, nokusika kwayo okuqondile. Bona [Hlola izinhlaka zokhonkolo nenza imithwalo ye-seismic](topic:concrete-and-seismic).
- **Okufanele umenjiniyela ahlole.** Imiphumela yezisekelo, yokhonkolo kanye ne-What breaks first iyizisizo kuphela. Imithwalo yomoya ayikakhiwa.

### Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Izilimi.** Usizo lutholakala ngezilimi ezingu-54. Ezingaphezu kwesiNgisi ziwumhlaba womshini kuze kube yilapho isipikha somdabu sizifunda; eziyishumi ziyizintsha (isiLithuanian, isiLatvian, isiBosnian, isiMarathi, isiTelugu, isiAlbanian, isiAfrikaans, isiFilipino, isiSwahili, isiUzbek).
- **Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Toolbar rows}} ikhetha ukugoqa, imigqa emi-2 noma umugqa oyi-1. Ibha esevele ilingana ihlala njalo; okunye kuskrola ngakwesokudla nakwesobunxele.
- **Ibhodi yezakhiwo.** Izinkambu zesisekelo zisebenzisa ibhodi evamile: izigaba ezingaqoqeka nobukhulu obuncane. Ukubala okubhaliwe njenge-2*450 kusasebenza.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** I-trackpad, ipeni, imouse ye-3D, isilawuli ne-MIDI kungashayela ama-app. Bona [Hamba ku-3D](topic:navigate-3d).
- **Ipaneli yezinkinga.** Izexwayiso zalezi zihlolo ezintsha zivela kuphaneli efanayo ethi {{ui:Problems}} njengakuqala.
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** Umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe, oxhunywe nge-CadCraft Studio, usebenza nazo zonke izinhlelo eziyisishiyagalombili futhi ubona okuvumela kuphela. Ukuxhuma ngenkinobho eyodwa, isixhumi esiphethwe, nokhiye wakho we-API ku-Studio kusengaphambili. Bona [Sebenzisa i-DetailCraft nomsizi we-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

## Kulungisiwe ku-0.8.1

- **Izilimi ku-Studio.** I-Studio nezinguqulo ezipakishiwe zingashintsha ulimi ngesikhathi somsebenzi; ngaphambili bekuyisiNgisi kuphela. Asikahlolwa kuwo wonke amapuleti: sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.
- **Ukukhetha ngewindi elidlula.** Abafakeli be-0.8.1 bathwele ukuziphatha kokukhetha ngewindi okwakuseqinisekile kakade kwisakhiwo sewebhu se-0.8; umfakeli owedlule wawungakwenzi lokho.
- **Amathebhulethi ngokuma.** Ukuphakama okusele kwegridi kuya ekhanvasini, nesakhiwo sephepha siqala ku-760 px noma ngaphansi.

- **Imithwalo yomoya** yokhonkolo ayikakhiwa, nezihlolo zokhonkolo nezisekelo zisemenjiniyeleni ukuze abuyekeze.
- **Ukusayina ikhodi.** Abafakeli be-Windows nefayela le-.dmg lwe-macOS aziyona esayiniwe, ngakho i-Windows ingalumba futhi i-macOS icela ukuthi uchofoze ngokudla bese uvula.
