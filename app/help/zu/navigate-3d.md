Ukubuka kunezimo ezintathu, ezikhethwa embhalweni wokubuka ngaphezulu kwayo: {{ui:3D}}, {{ui:Ipulani}} no-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}. Izinkinobho zemouse zenza izinto ezihlukile ku-3D kunku-Ipulani nasekubukekeni kohlangothi, ngakho funda zombili izinkomba.

## Ku-3D

:::mouse
| Ukuze | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Phendulela imodeli | Hudula ngenkinobho yesobunxele |
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngenkinobho yesokudla, noma bamba {{key:Shift}} uhudule ngenkinobho yesobunxele |
| Sondeza | Phendulela ivili, noma hudula phezulu nasezansi ngenkinobho ephakathi (ivili licindezelwe) |
| Khetha ingxenye | Chofoza kuyo (ukuchofoza okunyakaza ngaphansi kwamapikseli ambalwa) |
| Bona imodeli yonke | Inkinobho yokufaka esibonweni ekugcineni kwesokudla kwebha yokubuka |
:::

:::touch
| Ukuze | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Phendulela imodeli | Hudula ngomunwe owodwa |
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngeminwe emibili |
| Sondeza | Khama ngeminwe emibili |
| Khetha ingxenye | Thinta kuyo |
| Bona imodeli yonke | Inkinobho yokufaka esibonweni ekugcineni kwesokudla kwebha yokubuka |
:::

Ikhyubhu lokubuka ngakwesokudla phezulu (ngaphansi kwebha yokubuka) liphendulela ndawonye nemodeli: chofoza ubuso, umphetho noma ikhona ukuze ubheke kusuka lapho, hudula ukuze uphendule, noma chofoza inkinobho yalo yendlu ukuze ubuyele endaweni yokuqala. Bona [Ikhyubhu lokubuka](topic:view-cube).

Imodeli iphendulela phakathi kokubuka, nevili lisondeza ngasemaphakathini okubuka, hhayi ngasesikhombini. Ukuze ubheke ikhona eyodwa ngokucophelela, yishelelise iye phakathi kuqala, bese usondeza.

## Ku-Ipulani nokubukeka kohlangothi

Lezi zibheka ngqo imodeli futhi azikwazi ukuphendulelwa.

:::mouse
| Ukuze | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngenkinobho yesobunxele noma yesokudla |
| Sondeza | Phendulela ivili, noma hudula phezulu nasezansi ngenkinobho ephakathi |
| Khetha ingxenye | Chofoza kuyo |
:::

:::touch
| Ukuze | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngomunwe owodwa |
| Sondeza | Khama ngeminwe emibili (ukuhudula ngeminwe emibili nakho kuyashelelisa) |
| Khetha ingxenye | Thinta kuyo |
:::

## Ku-trackpad ye-Mac

:::mac
- **Sondeza:** swayiphela phezulu noma phansi ngeminwe emibili, noma khama. Kokubili kufinyelela ukubuka njengokusondeza.
- **Phendulela (3D):** cindezela i-trackpad uhudule ngomunwe owodwa. Uma usebenzisa tap to click, ukuthinta kabili nokuhudula nakho kuyasebenza uma ukuhudula kuvulwe ku **System Settings, Accessibility, Pointer Control, Trackpad Options**.
- **Shelelisa (3D):** bamba {{key:Shift}} bese uhudula. Ukubamba {{key:Ctrl}} noma {{key:Mod}} ngenkathi uhudula nakho kuyashelelisa. Ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili kungukuchofoza ngesokudla, kodwa ukuhudula ngakho akulula ku-trackpad.
- **Shelelisa (Ipulani nokubukeka kohlangothi):** hudula nje.
- I-Magic Mouse ayinayo inkinobho ephakathi: sebenzisa ukuhudula ngo-{{key:Shift}} ukushelelisa, nokuswayiphela ngaphezulu kwayo ukusondeza.
:::

Okwengeziwe ngokuchofoza ngesokudla nokushelelisa ngaphandle kwemouse: [Okhiye, amamouse namatrackpad](common:keys).

## Ipulani nokubukeka kohlangothi kwenza ukubeka kube lula

Ku-{{ui:3D}}, izixhumanisi ezinamazinga ahlukene zingahlala phezu komunye esikrinini, futhi ukuchofoza kuya kwelikhulu eliseduze. Ku-{{ui:Ipulani}} nakwi-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} kuphela amachashaza ezinga noma umugqa wegridi obukele kuwo angakhethwa.

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipulani}}, bese ukhetha izinga oludlule uhlu oluseduze kwezinkinobho. Ukubuka kubheka phansi kulelo zinga; okungaphezu kuka-1.5 m ngaphezulu kwalo kuyasikwa.
2. Noma khetha {{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}, bese ukhetha umugqa wegridi onamalitha ("Igridi A", "Igridi B" …). Ukubuka kubheka ngqo lowo mugqa; kukhonjiswa kuphela insimbi engaphakathi kuka-1 m kuwo.

Ukubukeka kohlangothi kutholakala kuphela emigqeni yegridi yamalitha. Ukusebenza emugqeni wegridi yamanani (uhlaka oluhamba kusuka ku-A kuya ku-B), sebenzisa {{ui:3D}}, noma beka ngokubhala efomini le-{{ui:Engeza ngegridi}}. Bona [Beka insika, amabhimu namabhulaketi](topic:place-members#place-by-grid-without-clicking).

## Ukukhetha

- Chofoza ingxenye ukuyikhetha. Iba ngombala okhanyayo futhi ephaneli ye-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} iyibonisa.
- Chofoza esikhaleni esingenalutho, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}}, ukuze usule ukukhethwa.
- Insimbi inqoba ukhonkrithi: ukuchofoza insika ngaphakathi kwesisekelo esibonakalayo ikhetha insika. Chofoza isisekelo ngaphandle kwensika, noma enye yezintonga zaso, ukuze ukhethe isisekelo.
- Ukuchofoza ibhawodi noma ipuleti lokuphela kukhetha ixhumanisi lalo.
- Ku-Ipulani nokubukeka kohlangothi, izingxenye ezisikiwe azikwazi ukuchofozwa.
- Bamba {{key:Shift}} (noma {{key:Ctrl}}, noma {{key:Mod}}) bese uchofoza ukuze wengeze ingxenye ekukhethweni noma uyisuse; {{key:Mod+A}} ikhetha konke. Akukho ukukhetha ngewindi (ukudonsa ibhokisi) okwamanje. Lokho ongakwenza nokuningi: [Kopisha, namathisela futhi uphinde](topic:copy-paste).

Ungakhetha futhi ezinhlwini: chofoza ukumaka ku-{{ui:Amamaki wengxenye}} ngakwesobunxele, ukumaka kwengxenye ethebhuleni le-{{ui:Uhlu lwempahla}}, noma {{ui:Khetha}} eduze komugqa ku-{{ui:Izinkinga}}.

## Uma ukubuka kubonakala kungalungile

- Ulahlekelwe yimodeli? Khetha inkinobho yokufaka esibonweni.
- Ukubuka kuyimpuphu noma kuluhlaza okunsundu nomlayezo onge-WebGL: bheka [Ukuxazulula izinkinga](topic:troubleshooting#the-3d-view-is-missing).
- Konke kusasebenza ngaphandle kokubuka ka-3D, ngaphandle kokuchofoza kuwo: amaphaneli, imibiko nokuthumela konke kuyasebenza, futhi i-{{ui:Engeza ngegridi}} ibeka izinto ngokubhala.
