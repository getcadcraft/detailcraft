Ipuleti lokuphela lixhuma ipuleti elishiselwe ekugcineni kwebhimu futhi libholiswe kwiflange noma kudonga lwensika. I-DetailCraft ibeka ipuleti, inqume ibhimu, ibhule izimbobo ezifanayo kupuleti nakwiflange yensika, ingeze amabhawodi nokushisela, futhi ihlole izikhala zemiphetho nokuhlukana.

:::warning
I-DetailCraft ayiklami izixhumanisi. Izilungiselelo ziyizinani zokuqala ozoziguqula; akukho okuhlola amandla. Izexwayiso zemiphetho nokuhlukana zisebenzisa ubuncane be-EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.3, obungahluka emithethweni osebenza ngayo.
:::

## Okungaxhumanisa

- Ibhimu **elilingene** (zombili izinhlangothi zalo zisezingeni elifanayo), elimile (rotation 0), neprofayili yesimo se-I.
- Kwiflange noma kudonga lensika **emile** yesimo se-I, ngaqondile kuyo.
- Umphetho webhimu kufanele ube kulayini wensika, phakathi kwangaphansi nangaphezulu kwensika.

Ibhimu elijikile noma elinamagongolo, nezigaba ezingesona ezesimo se-I zalahlwa nesizathu: sebenzisa [izinsimbi zokusika](topic:shear-tabs) kwalezo. Kudonga, ipuleti namafulenji ebhimu kufanele kulingane phakathi kwamafulenji ensika; i-DetailCraft iyaxwayisa uma engalingani.

:::tip
Ku-rotation 0 amafulenji ensika ajongana nendlela yegridi enezinombolo (x). Ibhimu elisuka ku-A liya ku-B lihlangana nodonga, futhi lithola ipuleti lokuphela kudonga.
:::

## Engeza amapuleti okuphela ebhimini

1. Khetha {{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:V}}) bese uchofoza ibhimu.
2. Khetha {{ui:Amaphuleti okuphela}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Xhuma}}, noma {{ui:Engeza amaphleti okuphela}} ephaneli le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}.
3. Yonke imiphetho ekhululekile yebhimu ehlangana neflange noma udonga lensika ngokuthe xaxa ithola ipuleti. Ibha yesimo ithi "Amapuleti okuphela angu-2 engeziwe. Khetha elilodwa ukuze ushintshe ipuleti, amabhawodi nokushisela kwalo; Okufanayo kulikopisha emahlanganweni afana nalo."
4. Ipuleti lokuphela lokuqala elisha liyakhethwa, nezilungiselelo zalo zisephaneli le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}.

Uma kungenzeki lutho, funda ibha yesimo:

- "Lolu bhimu alukaphumi ulayini wensika. Amapuleti okuphela abekwa lapho umphetho webhimu uhlangana nodonga lensika oluma."
- "Lolu bhimu lihlangana nensika nge-engeli noma lunomagongolo: amapuleti okuphela akhelwe amahlangano aqondile nalingene. Sebenzisa izinsimbi zokusika."
- "Zombili izinhlangothi zebhimu sezinexhumanisi."

## Shintsha ipuleti, amabhawodi nokushisela

Chofoza ipuleti lokuphela (noma elinye lamabhawodi alo) ukuze ukhethe ixhumanisi. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ipuleti, amabhawodi nokushisela (mm)}}:

| Ilungiselelo | Okuzenzakalelayo | Lokho akwenzayo |
|---|---|---|
| Ubukhulu bepuleti | 20 | Ubukhulu balo; futhi ibhimu linqunywa kangakanani ngaphezu kweflange |
| Ububanzi bepuleti | 180 | Kulo lonke uhlangano |
| Isandiso ngaphezu kwebhimu | 0 | Ngaphezu kweflange engaphezulu; ngaphezu kwe-0 kwenza ipuleti lokuphela elelulekile |
| Isandiso ngaphansi kwebhimu | 0 | Ngaphansi kweflange engaphansi |
| Ububanzi bebhawodi | 20 | Usayizi osemthethweni webhawodi |
| Ububanzi bembobo | 22 | Ukukhululeka kwakho kwembobo kupuleti nakweflange yensika |
| Ibanga lebhawodi | A325 | Umbhalo; uphrintwa kuhlu lwezinto |
| Imigqa yamabhawodi | 4 | Imigqa yamabhawodi amabili, ephakathi kobude bepuleti |
| Ukuhlukana kwemigqa | 75 | Ukuhlukana okuqondile phakathi kwemigqa |
| Igeyji | 100 | Ukuhlukana okuseceleni phakathi kwamabhawodi amabili emgqeni |
| Isilungiselelo sobude bebhawodi | 30 | Kungezwa ku-grip ukuze kubalwe unati, i-washer nomlinganiso; bheka ngezansi |
| Usayizi wokushisela | 8 | Konke ozungeze umphetho webhimu |
| Impahla yepuleti | A36 | Umbhalo |

Thayipha inani elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Tab}}. Ipuleti, izimbobo, amabhawodi nobude bebhimu elinqunyiwe kubuyekezwa ngokushesha. Ushintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

Ngezansi kwezilungiselelo ubona okwenziwa yixhumanisi:

- {{ui:Amabhawodi}}: isibonelo "8 × 20 × 70 A325 (grip 36.2)".
- {{ui:Ukushisela}}: usayizi, lapho, nobude bayo.
- {{ui:Ipuleti}}: usayizi walo nemaki yengxenye yalo.

### Indlela izinombolo ezibalwa ngayo

- Ibhimu linqunywa ngesigamu sokujula kwensika (kudonga: isigamu sobukhulu bodonga) kanye nobukhulu bepuleti, ukuze ipuleti lihlale ebusweni.
- Ubude bepuleti yijulile yebhimu kanye nezandiso ezimbili.
- Ubude bebhawodi = ukubamba (ubukhulu bepuleti + ubukhulu beflange noma bodonga lensika) + isilungiselelo, kuzungezwe kuya ku-5 mm elandelayo.
- Ubude bokushisela obuboniswayo ngumjikelezo wesigaba sebhimu.

### Izexwayiso

Ixhumanisi lisakhiwa, kodwa lezi zivela ngaphansi kwalo nakuthebhu ethi {{ui:Izinkinga}}:

- Imigqa yamabhawodi noma amabhawodi aseduze komphetho wepuleti kunoma 1.2 × ububanzi bembobo.
- Ukuhlukana kwemigqa okungaphansi kwe-2.2 × ububanzi bembobo, noma igeyji engaphansi kwe-2.4 × ububanzi bembobo.
- Izimbobo ezingena kudonga lensika noma kwi-fillet yalo, noma ephuma eflangeni lensika.
- Ipuleti elibanzi kuneflange yensika, noma (kudonga) elibanzi kunendawo phakathi kwamafulenji; amafulenji ebhimu angalingani phakathi kwawo.
- Umugqa wamabhawodi useduze kakhulu neflange yebhimu, noma igeyji incane kakhulu kudonga lwebhimu nokushisela kwalo.

## Kopisha kuwo wonke amahlangano afanayo

1. Khetha ixhumanisi olulungisile.
2. Khetha {{ui:Sebenzisa kokufanayo (n)}} ephaneli le-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}, noma {{ui:Okufanayo}} kwimenyu ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} yeqenjini le-{{ui:Xhuma}}. Inombolo ngaphakathi kwamabhulaketi yinani lamahlangano azoyithola.
3. Wonke ama-joint anephrofayili yebhimu efanayo nensika efanayo, ebhimu eliya ohlobo lobuso olufanayo, engenaxhumanisi okwamanje, athola ikhophi enezilungiselelo ezifanayo. Ibha yesimo ithi kangaki.

Amakhophi azimele: ukushintsha elilodwa ngemuva akushintshi ezinye. Amahlangano asenaxhumanisi ashiywa njengoba ekhona; sula lawo kuqala uma ufuna ukuwashintsha.

## Ukuhamba nokususa

Izixhumanisi zakhiwa kabusha ngezilungiselelo zazo njalo lapho imodeli ishintsha, ngakho ukuhambisa noma ukushintsha usayizi webhimu noma insika kuhambisa namapuleti awo okuphela. Uma ushintsho lusho ukuthi ukuhlangana akusafaneli, ixhumanisi lisahlala emodelini futhi ithebhu ethi {{ui:Izinkinga}} itshela ukuthi kungani, isibonelo "Umphetho webhimu awusaphansi kulayini wensika."

Ukususa ixhumanisi, khetha lona bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}}. Ukususa ibhimu noma insika kususa nezixhumanisi zalo.
