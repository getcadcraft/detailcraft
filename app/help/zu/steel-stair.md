Ijenereyitha yesitebhisi yenza isitebhisi sensimbi esiphelele ngezinombolo ezimbalwa: izixhasi, izinyathelo, izikhala zokuphumula uma indiza inde kakhulu, izibambelelo ezinezinsika namabhalasta, izimbobo zokubopha ezixhasini, uhlu lokusikwa nezisindo, nemidwebo yokukhiqiza. Iqala ngethuluzi {{ui:Stair}}, njengesitebhisi esilula (bona Izitebhisi), kanye nokuchofoza okukodwa okungeziwe.

## Beka isitebhisi sensimbi

1. Khetha {{ui:Stair}} eqenjini {{ui:Place}}, noma cindezela {{key:S}}.
2. Ngakwesokudla, thikha {{ui:Use the steel stair generator}}.
3. Khetha **isethi yemithetho** yendawo lapho isitebhisi sakhiwa, **ukuphakama** (phansi kuya phansi; 0 = kuya ezingeni elilandelayo), **ukuhamba okutholakalayo** (0 = vumela umthetho unqume), **ububanzi obuvulekile** kanye **nohlobo lwenyathelo**: iphuleti lepatheni, igreyithingi noma ipani elibhengiwe.
4. Setha isigaba sexhasi, amaphaseji (ngomthetho, noma inombolo), izibambelelo (ohlangothini lwombili, olulodwa, akukho), ukuphakama kwesibambelelo nesikhala samabhalasta.
5. Chofoza indawo yegridi: leyo yisisekelo senyathelo lokuqala.

Khetha isitebhisi (chofoza ixhasi, inyathelo noma isibambelelo) ukuze ushintshe noma yisiphi isilungiselelo; izakhiwo zikhombisa izinyathelo, ukuhamba, ukuthambeka, izindiza namaphaseji, insimbi ekuso, uhlu lokusikwa nemithombo yesethi yemithetho.

## Izethi zemithetho

Isethi yemithetho ikhawulela isimo sesitebhisi: ukuphakama nokuhamba, 2 × ukuphakama kwesinyathelo + ukuhamba, ukuthambeka, isikhala sokuphumula, ukuphakama kwesibambelelo nokuvulwa okukhulu kakhulu. I-DetailCraft inikeza lezi ezingezansi; isifunda siyisilungiselelo, hhayi ukuqagela. Vula **Lapho kuvela izinombolo zalesi sethi yemithetho** ephaneli yezakhiwo: inombolo ngayinye ibiza umthombo wayo futhi itshele ukuthi yahlolwa kahle kangakanani.

- **International Building Code 2021**: inyathelo 4 kuya ku-7 in., inyathelo elingaba ngu-11 in. ngaphansi, izibambelelo 34 kuya ku-38 in. Umkhawulo ongu-12 ft phakathi kwezikhala zokuphumula ukhonjiswa njengo *ongafundwanga*, nezinombolo zezibambelelo zifundwe emagcaleni akhomba ikhodi: ziqinisekise ngaphambi kokuthembela kuzo.
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.25 no-1910.29** (izitebhisi zemboni ezijwayelekile): 30° kuya ku-50°, inyathelo elingaphezu kwe-9.5 in., inyathelo elingaba ngu-9.5 in. ngaphansi, ububanzi obungaba ngu-22 in. ngaphansi, izikhala zokuphumula ezilingana nesitebhisi nezijula ngu-30 in., isibambelelo esiphezulu esisetshenziswa njengesibambelelo sesandla esi-36 kuya ku-38 in. Lokhu kufundwe emthethweni ngokwawo.
- **Approved Document K 2013, England** (izitebhisi zokufinyelela nezokusebenza ngokujwayelekile): izinhlu zokuphakama nokuhamba nama-2 × ukuphakama kwesinyathelo + ukuhamba 550 kuya ku-700 mm, zifundwe kumbiko obuyiselwe uhulumeni owazihlela. Lo mbhalo awunikezi i-DetailCraft ukuphakama kwesibambelelo, ngakho ukuphakama osikusethile kuyasetshenziswa futhi uhlu lwezinkinga luyakusho lokho.

Isethi yemithetho engakhona ayiqagelwa: isitebhisi silandela umthetho ovamile wokududuza ongu-600 kuya ku-650 mm bese sithi asinaso umthetho.

## Lokho esikwenzayo

- **Izinyathelo nokuhamba.** Okuncane kakhulu kwezinyathelo okugcina i-2 × ukuphakama kwesinyathelo + ukuhamba kuhlala phakathi kwesikhala somthetho, kungabi ukuhamba okungaphansi kwezinga elincane lomthetho. Uma kunikezwa ukuhamba, ukuhamba kuyinani lokuhamba kuzo zonke izinyathelo, futhi noma yini engaphandle komthetho iyashiwo.
- **Amaphaseji nezikhala zokuphumula.** Iphaseji elide kunalokho omthetho ovumela lihlukaniswe ngesikhala sokuphumula esilingana nesitebhisi futhi singaphansi kwezinga elincane lomthetho; ungacela futhi izikhala zokuphumula. Isikhala sokuphumula iyifreyimu yezinhlangothi ezimbili zebhimu nama-angle amabili aphambanayo ngaphansi kwesembozo, ezinsimbeni ezimbili.
- **Izinyathelo.** Iphuleti lepatheni liyiphuleti eshiselwe (6 mm ngokuzenzakalelayo; iphethini ayifakwa esisindweni). Ipani elibhengiwe linokubuya ku-nosing futhi libotshwe ngewebhu lexhasi: izimbobo ezimbili ezingu-2 mm ezinkulu kunebhawodi ku-0.3 nango-0.7 wobujula. Igreyithingi idwetshwa njengephuleti lesisindo esilinganayo (ubujula bebha yokusekela × ubukhulu ÷ ukuhlukana); izinti zokuphambanisa nobhande azibalwa, futhi uhlu lokusikwa luyasho lokho.
- **Izibambelelo.** Ipayipi elingaphezulu liyisibambelelo esiphakeme ezinsimbeni ezihamba nendiza ngayinye nasesikhaleni ngasinye sokuphumula, nesikhala samabhalasta esingavumeli ibhola elikhulu kunokuvulwa komthetho ukuthi lidlule (100 mm kakhulu ngokuzenzakalelayo).

## Imidwebo, uhlu lokusikwa, ukuthumela

- Indiza ingumhlangano owodwa; izibambelelo zingenye (zombili izinhlangothi zifana, zenziwe kabili). Ngayinye inomdwebo wayo wenhlanganisela, nomdwebo wexhasi ukhombisa izimbobo zawo.
- **Ishidi lesitebhisi** (ST-…) likhombisa ixhasi ngalinye ligqamile nezimbobo ezibhalwe kusuka ekugcineni okuphansi, nemininingwane yokunyathela: isigaba, i-nosing, ukubuya nendiza yokugcina enezimbobo zayo.
- {{ui:Cut list and weights}} ephaneli yezakhiwo ihlu zonke izigaba nobude nezinombolo nezikilogremu; {{ui:Download the cut list (CSV)}} iyakonga.
- Konke kuya ohlwini lwempahla, emafayeleni e-NC nasekuthunyelweni kwe-IFC njengezingxenye ezivamile (IfcMember, IfcColumn, IfcPlate).

## Okufanele wazi

- Lokhu kuyisiqalo, hhayi ukuklama. Akukho okuhlolwa ngamandla noma ngokuguga; isethi yemithetho ikhawulela kuphela isimo.
- Ukuphela kwezixhasi ngaphansi kwamafulowa kusikwa kwesikwele futhi akwenziwa ngemininingwane; izinsika zesikhala sokuphumula zimi kufulowa ongaphansi futhi azinamaphuleti esisekelo.
- Ukubeka isitebhisi njengento eyodwa ku-BimCraft akukaxhunyiwe okwamanje.
