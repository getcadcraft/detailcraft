## Okuzokwenziwa

Sebenza ngomlando wezindaba ofanayo nalowo wetimu yonke. Bona ukuthi yiziphi amashidi aphume ngobuqiniso ngokubuyekezwa kwawo, bese ugcina okwenzeka esayithini ngokuxhumene nendaba okuyiyo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula **Izindaba nokubuyekezwa**.
2. Khetha **Funda umlando wendaba** bese ukhetha ifayela `.issues.json` elilondolozwe ku-BimCraft noma enye i-app. Izindaba zalo nemirekhodo yalo yengezwa kwezakho. Akukho okushintshwa.
3. Khetha **Engeza irekhodi** lomyalo wesayithi, umdwebo wokukhiqiza, isethi yamamaki, imodeli exhunyiwe noma inothi. Khetha indaba okuyo nesimo sayo. Ifayela oliKhethile liqoshwa ngegama, usayizi kanye ne-SHA-256; ifayela lihlala lapho lisendaweni yalo.
4. **Uchungechunge lwezinguquko zomdwebo** lubonisa umugqa owodwa ngeshidi ngalinye nekholomu eyodwa ngendaba ngayinye. Ukubuyekezwa okusemabhulaketini kwakhishwa futhi akushintshiwe.
5. **Landa uchungechunge lwezinguquko zomdwebo (CSV)** ilondoloza izindaba, ithebula lamashidi namarekhodi. **Londoloza umlando wezindaba** ibhala ifayela ezinye ii-app ezilifundayo.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Amashidi asakhishwa kusuka ku-Imidwebo, ishidi ngalinye. Lezo zindaba zivela lapha: amashidi akhishelwe ngosuku olufanayo nenothi efanayo aba yindaba eyodwa.
- Amarekhodi nezindaba ozifundile zilondolozwa efayelweni lemodeli. Ushintsho ngalunye lulodwa lokuHlehlisa.
- Ukufunda ifayela elifanayo kabili akwengezi okuphindwe kabili. Lapho kukhona amakhophi amabili aqukethe irekhodi elifanayo, elentsha iyagcinwa.
