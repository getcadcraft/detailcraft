Izingxenye zibekwa ezindaweni zokuhlangana kwegridi: insika ngodwana, ibhimu noma ibhulaketi phakathi kwamabili. Setha [amagridi namazinga](topic:grids-and-levels) kuqala.

## Khetha ithuluzi nezinketho zalo

1. Khetha {{ui:Insika}} ({{key:C}}), {{ui:Ibhimu}} ({{key:B}}) noma {{ui:Ibhulaketi}} ({{key:R}}) eqenjini {{ui:Beka}}.
2. Ipaneli {{ui:Izakhiwo}} engakwesokudla iba "Beka insika" (noma ibhimu, noma ibhulaketi) ibonise izinketho zengxenye elandelayo:
   - {{ui:Iphrofayili}}: khetha kuhlu. Usayizi uhlelwe ngekhathalogi (ama-W shape, Amachaneli, Ama-engeli, HSS, IPE, HEA, HEB, UB, UC, PFC, EA, UEA, SHS, RHS, CHS, namaphrofayili wakho wezifiso). Bona [Ikhathalogi yeprofayili](topic:profile-catalog).
   - {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}}: A992, A36, A572 Gr 50, A500 Gr C, S235, S275 noma S355.
   - {{ui:Isikhundla}}: lapho isigaba sihlala khona emugqeni phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili: {{ui:Phezulu kwensimbi kumugqa}}, {{ui:Kumaphakathi komugqa}} noma {{ui:Phansi kumugqa}}.
   - {{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}}: iphendula isigaba ngokuzungeza i-eksisi yengxenye yaso.
   - {{ui:Kuze kube}} (amakholomu kuphela): {{ui:Izinga elilandelayo phezulu}}, noma igama lezinga.
3. Ngaphansi kwezinketho, ibhokisi {{ui:Iphrofayili}} libala izilinganiso zesigaba esikhethiwe, indawo, ubunzima ngemitha nekhathalogi esuka kuyo.

Okuzenzekelayo kwensika ngu-W12x50 ku-A992 (ekumaphakathi), kwebhimu ngu-W16x26 ku-A992 (phezulu kwensimbi emugqeni), nebhulaketi ngu-HSS6x6x1/4 ku-A500 Gr C (ekumaphakathi). Izinketho zihlala zisethiwe uze uzishintshe.

## Beka ngokuchofoza

### Insika

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipulani}} bese ukhetha izinga lapho insika iqala khona.
2. Khetha {{ui:Insika}}.
3. Hambisa isikhombi eduze kwendawo yokuhlangana. Iphoyinti eliseduze liyakhanya, futhi ibha yesimo iliqamba.
4. Chofoza. Insika ihamba isuka kulelo zinga iye ezingeni elilandelayo phezulu (noma ezingeni {{ui:Kuze kube}}). Ibha yesimo ithi, isibonelo, "Insika W12x50 ku-A/1, Izinga 1 kuya ku-Izinga 2."
5. Qhubeka uchofoza ukuze ubeke ezinye. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} uma usuqedile.

Uma kungekho zinga phezu kwelo ochofoze kulo, ibha yesimo ithi "Ayikho izinga phezu kwe-Izinga 2 insika okufanele ifike kulo. Engeza izinga kuqala."

### Ibhimu

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipulani}} bese ukhetha izinga lebhimu.
2. Khetha {{ui:Ibhimu}}.
3. Chofoza indawo yokuhlangana yokuqala. Ibha yesimo ithi "Kusuka ku-A/1 ku-Izinga 2. Manje chofoza esinye isiphetho ({{key:Esc}} iyakhansela)." Umugqa ohlanganisiwe ulandela isikhombi.
4. Chofoza indawo yokuhlangana yesibili. Ibhimu liyavela.
5. Chofoza futhi ukuze uqale ibhimu elilandelayo, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}}.

{{key:Esc}} ngemva kokuchofoza kokuqala ikhansela leyo ndawo kuphela; {{key:Esc}} yesibili ibeka ithuluzi eceleni.

### Ibhulaketi

Ibhulaketi lihamba phakathi kwezindawo ezimbili zokuhlangana ezinamazinga ahlukene, ngakho lilula kakhulu ku-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}.

1. Khetha {{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} bese ukhetha umugqa wegridi onombolo ibhulaketi elisemugqeni wawo.
2. Khetha {{ui:Ibhulaketi}}.
3. Chofoza isiphetho esingaphansi, bese isiphetho esiphezulu.

Ukubukeka kohlangothi kukhona kuphela kumamugqa egridi anezinhlamvu. Ibhulaketi ohlakeni oluhamba ngomugqa onombolo (kusuka ku-A kuya ku-B), sebenzisa {{ui:3D}} noma ifomu {{ui:Engeza ngegridi}} elingezansi.

:::touch
Esikrinini sokuthinta, thinta eduze kwendawo yokuhlangana: ukuthinta kukhetha iphoyinti eliseduze uma likhona eduze kakhulu, futhi akwenzi lutho uma lingekho eduze. Akukho ukukhanya ngaphambi kokuthinta, ngakho hlola umlayezo webha yesimo ngemva kokuthinta ngakunye. Ukudonsa ngomunwe owodwa kujikeleza noma kuhambisa umbono esikhundleni sokubeka.
:::

## Beka ngegridi, ngaphandle kokuchofoza

Yonke ithuluzi lokubeka linefomu {{ui:Engeza ngegridi}} ngaphansi kwepaneli {{ui:Izakhiwo}}. Lisebenza ngekhibhodi kuphela futhi alidingi umbono we-3D.

1. Khetha ithuluzi, isibonelo {{ui:Ibhimu}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Engeza ngegridi}}, khetha {{ui:Kusuka egridini (uhlamvu)}}, {{ui:Kusuka egridini (inombolo)}} kanye {{ui:Kusuka kwizinga}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Kuya egridini (uhlamvu)}}, {{ui:Kuya egridini (inombolo)}} kanye {{ui:Kuya kwizinga}}.
4. Khetha {{ui:Engeza}}.

Ngensika noma isisekelo ifomu linephoyinti elilodwa kuphela: {{ui:Igridi (uhlamvu)}}, {{ui:Igridi (inombolo)}} kanye {{ui:Izinga}}.

## Shintsha ingxenye ngemva kokuyibeka

1. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} (noma khetha {{ui:Khetha}}, {{key:V}}) bese uchofoza ingxenye.
2. Epaneli {{ui:Izakhiwo}}, shintsha {{ui:Igama}}, {{ui:Iphrofayili}}, {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}}, {{ui:Isikhundla}} noma {{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}}.
3. Ukuze uyihambise, hlela x, y no-z ka-{{ui:Ukuqala}} noma {{ui:Ukuphela}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Imiphetho (x, y, z mm)}}. Igama legridi lesiphetho ngasinye livela ngaphansi kwaso, noma izixhumanisi zaso uma singaphezu kwegridi.

Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ngaphansi kwezinkambu ubona {{ui:Imaka yengxenye}}, {{ui:Ukuhlanganisa}}, {{ui:Ubude bokusika}}, {{ui:Isisindo}} (esigcwele: izimbobo namakhona akususwa) kanye nekhathalogi iphrofayili eyaziwa kuyo.

Izingxenye azikwazi ukudonswa noma zihlelwe ngohlu okwamanje. Ukwenza enye efana nayo, yikopishe bese uyinamathisela, noma uyiphinde ({{key:Mod+D}}): bona [Kopisha, namathisela futhi uphinde](topic:copy-paste).

## Susa ingxenye

Yikhethe bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}}, noma khetha {{ui:Susa}}. Uxhumano olubhekele kuyo lunamathela nalo. {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisela konke.

## Okufanele wazi

- **Ukuthi insika ibhekiswe kanjani.** Ku-{{ui:Ukujikeleza (°)}} 0, amaflenji ensika abheke ngase-x, ngasemigqeni yegridi enombolo ecaleni. Ibhimu elisuka ku-grid 1 liya ku-grid 2 lihlanganisa efulenjini; ibhimu elisuka ku-A liya ku-B lihlanganisa ewebini. Ama-end plate akhiwa kumaflenji kuphela, ngakho uma amabhimu akho esuka ku-A kuya ku-B, nika leyo nsika ukujikeleza okungu-90.
- **Amabhimu asezingeni ehlala ngaphansi kwalo** ngesikhethelo esizenzekelayo {{ui:Phezulu kwensimbi kumugqa}}. Khetha {{ui:Kumaphakathi komugqa}} uma amazinga akho enkomba zezingxenye zawo.
- **Izinsika zihamba izinga ngezinga.** Insika edlula kwezimbili zezitezi, yenza {{ui:Kuze kube}} ibe yizinga eliphezulu.
- **Amabhimu athambekile** ayavunyelwa (khetha iziphetho ezinamazinga ahlukene), kodwa ama-end plate awakakhiwi kuwo.

Okulandelayo: [Xhuma amabhimu nezinsika ngama-end plate](topic:end-plates).
