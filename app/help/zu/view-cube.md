Ikhyubhu lokubuka lisekhoneni elingaphezulu kwesokudla sokubuka kwe-3D, ngaphansi kwebha yokubuka (kuphela ku-{{ui:3D}}: {{ui:Ipulani}} ne-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} sebejwayele ukubuka imodeli ngqo). Liyaphendukela ngokubuka, ngakho lihlala likhombisa ukuthi ubheka kuphi: {{ui:Phezulu}} ingaphezulu, {{ui:Phambili}} ibheka eningizimu (uhlangothi amagridi amagama abonakala ngalo ku-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}), {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}} ibheka empumalanga, ngasemgqeni wamagridi anamanani. Ihlala ibonakala ize uyilobe.

## Buka ngohlangothi, ngomphetho noma ngekhoni

1. Lobela ikhyubhu. Ingxenye ephansi kwesikhombi ikhanyisa: uhlangothi ophakathi, umphetho (ukhanyisa kumahlangothi amabili ahlanganyela wona) noma ikhoni (kumahlangothi amathathu).
2. Chofoza. Ukubuka kuphendukela ukubheka kusuka lapho, kugcinwe maphakathi nokusondela okufanayo.

Kukhona izindawo ezingu-26 ongachofoza kuzo: izinhlangothi eziyi-6 ({{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phansi}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Emuva}}, {{ui:Ngakwesobunxele}}, {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}}), imiphetho eyi-12 namakhoni angu-8. Ikhoni linika ukubuka okuwu-three-quarter; umphetho ubukeka kuphakathi kwezinhlangothi ezimbili.

Ukubuka kohlangothi lwekhyubhu kuyisibuko se-3D, hhayi {{ui:Ipulani}} noma {{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}: akukho okusikiwe, futhi imodeli isengazungezwa.

## Zungeza ngokudonsa ikhyubhu

Donsa ikhyubhu ukuze uphendule ukubuka, njengokuzungeza ngaphakathi kokubuka ngokwakho. Ukudonsa akuthathwa njengokuchofoza, ngakho ungabamba ikhyubhu noma kuphi.

## Isiyingi sekhampasi

Isiyingi esingaphansi kwekhyubhu sinamagama N, E, S no-W.

- **Donsa isiyingi** ukuze uphendule imodeli ngomugqa oqondile, njengokuthi imi ethafeni. Isiyingi silandela isikhombi.
- **Chofoza unhlamvu** ukuze ubheke kuleyo ndlela, kugcinwe ukuthi ubheka phansi kangakanani. Ekubukeni okuqondile phezulu, unhlamvu oyichofozile ufika ezansi kwesikrini.

## Imibukwa ebheke ubuso: izimpuku

Lapho ubheka ubuso ngqo, izimpuku ezincane zivela zizungeze ikhyubhu:

- **Izikhonkwane ezinxantathu** zizisa ubuso bolwo hlangothi phambili: inxantathu ngaphezulu kwekhyubhu ikhombisa ubuso obuvele buphezulu, kanjalo nakwezinye.
- **Izimpuku ezigobile ezingakwesokudla phezulu** zizungeza isithombe ingxenye yokuzungeza ngasekhlokweni noma ngasebunxeleni, ngaphandle kokushintsha ubuso obubhekwayo. Zisebenzise ukujikeleza ipulani ukuze udonga olude lugqame kuskrini.

## Ekhaya

Inkinobho yendlu esekhoneni eliphezulu lwesobunxele lekhyubhu ibuyela ekubukeni kokuqala okuwu-three-quarter futhi ifake imodeli yonke, njengenkinobho yokufaka. {{key:Home}} yenza okufanayo lapho ikhyubhu linokugxila kwekhibhodi.

## Ngekhibhodi

{{key:Tab}} uye kukhyubhu, bese:

| Ukhiye | Yenza |
|---|---|
| {{key:left}} / {{key:right}} | khetha uhlangothi, umphetho noma ikhoni ezungeze ikhyubhu, ngezinyathelo zesigamu sesine sokuzungeza (abafundi besikrini bayasho ukuthi yiliphi) |
| {{key:up}} / {{key:down}} | khetha okuphezulu noma okuphansi, kuze kufike ku-{{ui:Phezulu}} noma kube ku-{{ui:Phansi}} |
| {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Space}} | buka kusuka kolukhethiwe |
| {{key:Shift+left}} / {{key:Shift+right}} | phendula ukubuka ngesigamu sesine sokuzungeza ngokuzungeza kwe-axis eqondile |
| {{key:Home}} | ukubuka kwasekhaya |
| {{key:Esc}} | shiya ikhyubhu |

Okhiye acindezelwe ekhyubhu bahlala ekhyubhini: abaqali amathuluzi e-DetailCraft.

## Isithonjana se-axis noma lutho

Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), ithebhu ye-{{ui:DetailCraft}}, bese usetha **Isikhombisi sekhoni** ku-{{ui:Ikhyubhu lokubuka}} (okuqala), {{ui:Isithonjana se-axis}} (isithonjana esincane se-X, Y, Z ngaphansi kwesobunxele: i-X ibomvu, i-Y iluhlaza, i-Z ibhakabhaka) noma {{ui:Lutho}}. Lesi sisakazi sikhumbula ukukhetha. Bona [izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings#corner-indicator).
