Isitebhisi sensimbi esiqondile: izixhasi ezimbili (amachannel anezinzwayi zawo zingena ngaphakathi, noma amabha acaba emaphethelweni ahlanganiswe ngokuma), nenyathelo lephuleti ngalinye elishiselwe phakathi kwawo. I-DetailCraft ibala inani lezinyathelo kusukela ekuphakameni, ihlola ngemikhawulo yekhodi yokwakha, bese yenza isitebhisi sibe yisakhiwo esisodwa semboni.

## Beka isitebhisi

1. Khetha {{ui:Stair}} eqenjini {{ui:Place}}, noma cindezela {{key:S}}.
2. Setha isitebhisi ngakwesokudla: **indlela yokukhuphuka** (0° ikhuphuka ecaleni kwe-+x, 90° ecaleni kwe-+y), **ukuphakama** (0 = kuya ezingeni elilandelayo), **ukuhamba**, **ububanzi obuvulekile** phakathi kwezixhasi, **inyathelo eliphezulu elivunyelwe**, **ixhasi**, **ubukhulu lwephuleti lokunyathela**, **i-nosing** nempahla.
3. Chofoza indawo yegridi: leyo yisisekelo senyathelo lokuqala, ezansi kwesikhundla sendawo yesitebhisi.

Ibha yesimo ithi zingaki izinyathelo ezenziwe. Khetha isitebhisi (chofoza ixhasi noma inyathelo) ukuze ushintshe noma yisiphi isilungiselelo sayo, isikhundla noma isigaba sayo; izakhiwo zikhombisa futhi izinyathelo, amakhathazo, ukuhamba, ukuthambeka nama-2 × ukuphakama kwesinyathelo + ukuhamba.

## Okwenzayo i-DetailCraft

- **Izinyathelo.** Okuncane okugcina ngalinye kungaphansi noma kulingane nalokho okuphakeme: ukuphakama okungu-3000 mm ku-178 mm noma ngaphansi kunika izinyathelo ezingu-17 ezingu-176.5 mm. Kukhona inyathelo elincane kunezinyathelo (isitezi esiphezulu singelokugcina).
- **Okunyathela.** Iphuleti elijulile ngohlangothi lokuhamba + i-nosing (280 + 25 = PL6x305 ngokuzenzakalelayo) futhi lelide njengobubanzi obuvulekile, ophezulu walo kuwo wonke amaphakama enyathelo, nomphetho walo wangaphambili (i-nosing) phambi komugqa wesinyathelo.
- **Izixhasi.** Ngendlela yesitebhisi, kusukela lapho zihlangana nesitezi esingaphansi kuya lapho zihlangana nesitezi esingaphezulu, ngaphandle kobubanzi obuvulekile; umphetho wazo ophezulu ungu-50 mm ngaphezulu komugqa wama-nosing. Izinzwayi zechannel zibheka ngaphakathi kwesitebhisi; ibha ecaba efana ne-PL12x250 imi ngokubeka eceleni.
- **Ukushisela.** Inyathelo ngalinye kuzo zombili izixhasi. Izinyathelo nexhasi lesokudla zishiselwe exhasini lesobunxele, ngakho isitebhisi sithola imaki yenhlanganisela eyodwa (ST1) nomdwebo wayo wenhlanganisela; izixhasi zithola amamaki engxenye st…, izinyathelo p….
- **Ukuhlola.** Izexwayiso lapho inyathelo lingaphandle kuka-4 kuya ku-7 in. (102 kuya ku-178 mm) noma ukuhamba kungaphansi kuka-11 in. (279 mm), imikhawulo ye-IBC 2021 isigaba 1011.5.2, lapho 2 × ukuphakama kwesinyathelo + ukuhamba kungaphandle kuka-600 kuya ku-650 mm (umthetho womkhuba ovamile wokududuza), nalapho ixhasi lijulile kancane kakhulu ukuthi lithwale izinyathelo. Igama elingaziwa lephrofayili yexhasi livimbela isitebhisi ekwakhiweni.

## Okufanele wazi

- Ukuhamba, ukujikeleza, ukubonisa, ukukopisha, ukunamathisela, ukususa nezigaba kusebenza esitebhisini njengasezintweni. Ukujikeleza kuphendula ukukhuphuka; ukubonisa kuyithumela endleleni ebonisiwe.
- Izixhasi ziyathunyelwa emafayeleni e-IFC njenge-IfcMember, nezinyathelo njenge-IfcPlate; zombili ziya emafayeleni e-NC nohlwini lwempahla. Ukuhlola ukungqubuzana kushiya izingxenye zesitebhisi zodwa (ziyashiselwe ndawonye).
- Lesi sitebhisi esilula siyindiza eyodwa ngezinyathelo zephuleti. Ngamaphaseji, izikhala zokuphumula, izibambelelo namabhalasta, izinyathelo ezigoqiwe noma zegreyithingi, isethi yemithetho oyikhethayo, uhlu lokusikwa nemidwebo yezimbobo zexhasi, khetha {{ui:Use the steel stair generator}} kumsebenzi {{ui:Stair}} (bona Ijenereyitha yesitebhisi sensimbi).
- Izixhasi zisikwa zisquare: imiphetho yazo yokusika ezindongeni ezingaphansi ayenziwa.
- I-DetailCraft ayiklami isitebhisi: izixhasi nezinyathelo azihlolwa ngamandla noma ngokuguga.
