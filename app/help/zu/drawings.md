Khetha {{ui:Imidwebo}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Okukhiphayo}}. I-DetailCraft yenza sonke isethi kusuka emodelini njengoba ikhona manje. Akukho okugcinwayo: shintsha imodeli, vula {{ui:Imidwebo}} futhi, bese isethi yenziwa kabusha.

## Okufakiwe kwisethi

- **Amapulani**, eyodwa ngomugqa ngamugqa onento kuwo, abheka phansi.
- **Ukubukeka kohlangothi**, eyodwa ngomugqa wegridi ngamunye okukhona amalunga angaphakathi kwe-1 m kuwo.
- **Ipulani lamabhawodi okubamba**, uma imodeli inamapuleti esisekelo: wonke amapuleti esisekelo abonwa ngaphezulu nensika nezinti zokubamba zawo, izigridi namanani azo, incazelo ejwayelekile yephethini ngayinye yezinti (BP1, BP2 …) nesikhala sezinti nemikhawulo yemiphetho, kanye nezinhlu ezimbili: izinti zokubamba ngemaki (R1, R2 …: inani, ububanzi, ubude, ukuphakama ngaphezu kwepuleti, ukungena, impahla) namapuleti esisekelo ngegridi (insika, iphethini, ipuleti, izinti, phezulu kwepuleti). Insika engaphandle kwegridi ibekwa ngamakhonjana wayo.
- **Imidwebo yokuhlanganisa**, eyodwa ngemaki yokuhlanganisa ngayinye: imibono yangaphambili, yaphezulu nasesiphethweni, izilinganiso ezilandelanayo kumbobo nemiphetho yamapuleti, uhlu lwezingxenye, amabhawodi nezinti ezingahlanganiswanga, namanothi okushisela nezimbobo. Ipuleti ngalinye elishiselwe linophawu lwe-fillet ekubukeni kwangaphambili (AWS A2.4): umcibisholo uphawula ukuhlangana, unxantathu ongaphansi komugqa wesethenjwa usho uhlangothi lomcibisholo, inombolo ngakwesokunxele kwawo iwubukhulu bokushisela ngu-mm, nesiyingi lapho umcibisholo uhlangana nomugqa usho ukushisela konke.

**Imigqa efihliwe.** Amapulani nokubukeka kohlangothi kushiya imigqa yengxenye lapho ingxenye ephelele phambili kwayo iyifihla (ibhimu elibukwa ngqo ngemuva kensika). Imidwebo yokuhlanganisa idweba ipuleti ngemigqa emincane lapho ingxenye enkulu iyifihla: ngemuva kodonga ekubukeni kwangaphambili (kubukwa kusuka ohlangothini −u lwengxenye), ngaphansi kwe-flange engaphezulu ekubukeni okuphezulu. Izingxenye ezinzulu ezifanayo, noma ezingena kuzo ngokuhlanganayo, zidwetshwa ziphelele zombili.

Yonke ishidi linebhulokhi yesihloko enenombolo yomdwebo, isikali, igama lemodeli, usuku, inombolo yeshidi nosayizi weshidi, kanye nesethenjwa lemodeli: ikhodi eyenziwe kumodeli elondoloziwe, ukuze iphrinti lihlanganiswe nemodeli elivela kuyo.

## Khetha usayizi weshidi

Khetha usayizi ohlwini oluphezulu kwewindi le-{{ui:Imidwebo}}: {{ui:A4}}, {{ui:A3}}, {{ui:A2}}, {{ui:A1}}, {{ui:ANSI B}} (11 × 17 in), {{ui:ANSI C}} (17 × 22 in) noma {{ui:ANSI D}} (22 × 34 in). Wonke amashidi athe xaxa. Isethi yenziwa kabusha ngosayizi omusha ngokushesha, futhi usayizi ofanayo usetshenziswa nemidwebo yomsebenzi. Usayizi usendaweni ku-[izilungiselelo ze-DetailCraft](topic:settings#drawings), futhi le bhrawuza iyawukhumbula.

- Ishidi elikhulu lidweba ngesikali esikhulu. Izikali zihlala zingezijwayelekile (1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50, 1:100 njalo njalo).
- Umbhalo uhlala ungubukhulu obufanayo kuwo wonke amashidi, ukuze ufundeke lapho uphrintwa.
- Ekashidini elincane, i-assembly ende nemincane ingaba nesikali esikhulu ngobukhulu kunesikali ngobude. Isihloko sombono sibe sisho womabili amasikali, futhi izilinganiso ziyabusa.

## Khipha imidwebo nokuguqulwa

Umugqa ongaphezulu kwesibonelo utshela ukuthi ishidi elibonisiwe limi kanjani: **Akukakhishwa**, **Rev B, ikhishiwe … ; akushintshile kusukela-ke**, noma, ngombala obomvu, **Kushintshe kusukela rev B: kudinga ukukhishwa kabusha**. Uhlu lwamashidi luphawula amashidi akhishiwe ngokuguqulwa kwawo namashidi adinga ukukhishwa kabusha.

- Bhala ukuthi ukukhishwa kwenzelani (kuqala kuthi "Ikhishelwe ukukhiqizwa") bese ukhetha {{ui:Khipha leli shidi}}, noma {{ui:Khipha amashidi ashintshiwe}} ngamashidi wonke asha noma ashintshe kusukela ekukhishweni kwawo kokugcina.
- Ukukhishwa kokuqala kweshidi kungukuguqulwa A, bese B, C njalo njalo (I no-O ayiphindwa).
- Isihloko sebhulokhi sikhombisa inombolo yomdwebo ngokuguqulwa kwawo ("A-B1  rev B"), nethebula lokuguqulwa eseceleni liyakhombisa ukukhishwa okusha kakhulu: ukuguqulwa, usuku nencazelo. Ekashidini elincane likhombisa okuningi okulingana nendawo.
- Ishidi "liyashintsha" uma okudwetshiwe kulo kushintsha: ilunga elisunyiwe, imbobo entsha, imaki eshintshiwe kabusha. Usuku, inani lamashidi nethebula lokuguqulwa ngokwabo akubalwa. Ukushintsha usayizi weshidi kushintsha wonke amashidi.
- Irekhodi lokukhishwa ligcinwa efayeleni lemodeli, futhi ukukhipha kuyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa. [Ifayela le-KISS](topic:kiss-export) lithwala ukuguqulwa kokugcina kwemidwebo ngayinye.

### Amafu okuguqula

Kusukela ekukhishweni kwesibili kweshidi kuya phezulu, yonke ingxenye noma isisekelo esishintshile sidwetshwa ngaphakathi kwefu lokuguqula elinonxantathu olunohlamvu.

- Uma ishidi lishintshile kusukela ekukhishweni kwalo kokugcina, amafu akhombisa lokho okushintshile kusukela ngalokho, nonxantathu unohlamvu lokuguqula olulandelayo (rev B ikhishiwe futhi ishintshile: onxantathu bathi C).
- Uma selikhishiwe, amafu ahlala kwishidi lalokho kuguqulwa, njengesethi ephrintiwe, kuze kube yilapho ishidi lishintsha futhi.
- Ishidi ngalinye lifaka amafu kuphela ezinguquko alikhombisayo. Amapulani nokubukeka kohlangothi akudwetshi zimbobo noma ama-cope, ngakho afaka amafu ilunga elisunyiwe, elijikelezisiwe noma elilungiswe ubukhulu, noma elishintsha imaki yalo. Imidwebo yokuhlanganisa nepulani lamabhawodi okubamba nayo ifaka amafu izimbobo nama-cope amasha noma asunyiwe.
- Inombolo entsha yemaki iyabalwa: uma ushintsho lwenza ibhimu libe ukuhlanganiswa okusha, amabhimu ayo imaki yawo ekhuphuke (B1 kuya ku-B2) afakwa amafu emapulanini nasekubukekeni kohlangothi.
- Ilunga elisusiwe alisali lutho lokufaka amafu.
- Amashidi akhishwe ngaphambi kwamafu okuguqula (DetailCraft 0.4) aqala ukufaka amafu ekukhishweni kwawo okulandelayo.
- Amafu aba khona emidwebeni ye-PDF, DXF ne-DWG, futhi awabalwa njengoshintsho ngokwawo.

## Landa

Khetha ifomethi ohlwini oluseceleni kwezinkinobho, bese ukhetha {{ui:Leli shidi}} weshidi elibonisiwe noma {{ui:Wonke amashidi}} ngesethi yonke.

- **PDF**: ukuphrinta noma ukuthumela. {{ui:Wonke amashidi}} yenza i-PDF eyodwa, ikhasi elilodwa ngeshidi ngalinye, ngalinye lisayizi okhethiwe.
- **DWG**, ngefomethi ye-2018, 2013, 2010, 2007, 2004 noma 2000: ukuvula amashidi ohlelweni lwe-CAD. Khetha ifomethi endala uma uhlelo lwakho ludala.
- **DXF (R12)**: inguqulo ohlelo lwe-CAD oluningi luyivula.

Ishidi le-DWG noma le-DXF lidwetshwa ngobukhulu obugcwele: iyunithi yomdwebo eyodwa ingu-millimeter eyodwa ephepheni, ngakho iphrinta ngo-1:1 futhi yonke imibono igcina isikali esitshelwa isihloko sayo. Ipeni ngalinye liyisendlalelo sayo: DC-OUTLINE, DC-THICK, DC-THIN, DC-HIDDEN, DC-CENTER, DC-GRID njalo njalo, nobukhulu bomugqa (DWG) nezigxobo zalo. Amagama ayitexthi esitayela se-DetailCraft (Arial). Izilinganiso ziyimigqa nezinombolo ozibonayo, hhayi izinto zezilinganiso. {{ui:Wonke amashidi}} ifaka amafayela e-DWG noma e-DXF ku-zip eyodwa, ifayela elilodwa ngeshidi ngalinye.

:::note
I-DXF R12 ayinabukhulu bemigqa nokugcwaliswa: izendlalelo zigcina amagama azo, ngakho setha amapeni ngendlalelo lapho uphrinta, futhi izimo ezigcwele ziyasuswa (ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi zingaki).
:::

Phrinta ku-100% (hhayi "fit to page") ephepheni losayizi ofanayo uma ufuna izikali ezishiwo zihlale zisebenza.
