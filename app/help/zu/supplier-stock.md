Ithebhu {{ui:Ukuhlinzeka}} ibheka ngayinye yezigaba {{ui:Uhlu lokusika}} oluyithengayo ohlwini lwesitoko somhlinzeki. Ifayela lihlala emodelini; akukho lutho oluthunyelwa noma kuphi.

## Engeza ifayela lomhlinzeki

Cindezela {{ui:Engeza ifayela lomhlinzeki}} bese ukhetha i-CSV evela komhlinzeki wakho (ihlukaniswe ngekhoma, ngomcoloni noma ngethebhu). Idinga ikholomu yento noma isigaba (Item, Name, Description, Section, SKU), kanye neyesitoko (In stock, Quantity, On hand) noma eyesikhathi sokulethwa. Iyunithi (each, metres, kilograms, tonnes), intengo, umhlinzeki kanye nosuku lokugcwalisa kabusha kufundwa uma kukhona ikholomu yakho. Amagama nawo ayaqondwa: "Out of stock", "In stock", "2-3 weeks", "backorder". Kungagcinwa kuze kube amafayela amahlanu; ukwengeza ifayela negama elifanayo kuyalishintsha.

## Okubonwayo

Ngayinye yezigaba uhlu lokusika oluyithengayo, ithebhu iveza okudingwayo yimodeli, isitoko esikhona, isikhathi sokulethwa, umhlinzeki kanye nomphumela:

- **Kuneleyo:** kukhona isitoko esanele.
- **Kuyashoda ngo:** isitoko siphansi kunokudingekayo. Uma isitoko siphansi kunokudingekayo futhi usethe isikhathi eside kakhulu sokulinda, isikhathi sokulethwa sikutshela ukuthi okusele kuthatha isikhathi esingakanani.
- **Sekwephuzile:** akukho inani lesitoko, futhi isikhathi sokulethwa side kunokulinda okwamukelayo.
- **Akukho inani lesitoko:** ifayela alitshongo.
- **Enye iyunithi:** umhlinzeki ubhala isigaba eyunithini imodeli engayinikeza.

Okudingwa yimodeli kunikezwa ngamabha (kusuka ohlwini lokusika olufakiwe, ngobude bakho besitoko), ngamamitha ebha olithengwayo nangamakhilogremu (isisindo sesigaba ngemitha), kusetshenziswa iyunithi yefayela. Isigaba sifanisa ngegama laso ("W16 X 26" kanye "w16x26" kuyafana). Izigaba ezingekho ohlwini ziyadwetshwa.

Okushoda nezigaba ezephuzile kusohlwini {{ui:Izinkinga}}. Lapha akukho lutho oluyalwa.

## Imingcele

Izinani lesitoko nezikhathi zokulethwa ngokwefayela, njengoba kwakunjalo ngosuku lokwenziwa kwalo. Amapuleti awafakwa esakhiweni, ngakho awafunwa. Ifayela lifundwa kuphela: noma yiziphi izimiyalo ezikuwo ngumbhalo nje.
