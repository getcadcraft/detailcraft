## Lokho ozokufunda

Lokho okwenzeka kumodeli lapho abantu abaningi behlela ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqala futhi ijoyine iseshini ye-Live TeamWork (Yabelana, Joyina); leli khasi lichaza imodeli uqobo.

## Wonke umuntu usebenza ngesikhathi esisodwa

Engeza futhi uhlele izingxenye, ukuxhumana, izisekelo, izitebhisi nezikhwele njengoba kujwayelekile. Ushintsho lwakho luvela ngokushesha; ushintsho lwabanye luvela ngemva kwesikhashana ngemva kokuthi bakwenze.

- **Abantu ababili beshintsha ingxenye eyodwa:** izilungiselelo ezihlukene (enye iphrofayili, enye iphuzu lokuphela) zombili ziyagcinwa; isilungiselelo esifanayo siya kowokugcina ukushintsha.
- **Ukuxhumana kwengxenye umuntu asanda kuyisusa** kuyathathwa, futhi ibha yesimo ichaza ngesizathu.
- Amagridi, amazinga nezigaba kuya kowokugcina ukukushintsha.
- Ijometri eyakhiwe, amaphawu engxenye nohlu lwezinto kubalwa kuwo wonke amawindi kusuka kumodeli efanayo, ngakho ayavumelana.

## Bonana

Wonke umuntu kuseshini unombala wakhe. Ubona ikhesa ngalinye lomuntu njengomcibisholo onombala wakhe negama lakhe eseceleni, esingxenyeni noma kuphleti aqondise kulo, nebhokisi elibhalwe ngombala wakhe ezungeze izingxenye, ukuxhumana, izisekelo nezitebhisi azikhethile.

Izikhombisi, amagama nokukhethwa kuseshini kuphela: azigcinwa kufayela ngakanye futhi azingeni ku-undo yomuntu. Ziyashelela ziye lapho kukhona umuntu, ngakho zihlala zilula nakuxhumano oluhamba kancane.

## Ukuhlehlisa kwenza umsebenzi wakho kuphela

{{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa ushintsho lwakho lokugcina futhi ishiye ushintsho lwabanye lungathintwa. Uma kukhona umuntu oshintshe into efanayo kusukela lapho, akukho okuhlehliswayo futhi ibha yesimo ithi ngubani.

## Joyina kusiphequluli

Umuntu ongenaye i-Studio angaskena isithombe ngaphansi kwekhodi yomphathi ngefoni, ithebulethi noma ikhompyutha kuleyo nethiwekhi efanayo. I-DetailCraft ivuleka kusiphequluli futhi igcwale ngemodeli yomphathi uma umphathi evumela ukungena. Isixhumanisi sokumemela sivumela umuntu ukuthi ajoyine kusuka kwenye inethiwekhi: umphathi udinga isihlalo se-Team Cloud Plan ye-app, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi sisebenza kuphela uma iseshini yomphathi ivulekile.

## Lapho iseshini iphela

Ngumphathi kuphela ogcina imodeli. Abanye bonke bagcina ikhophi engagcinwanga: yigcine uma ufuna ukuyigcina.
