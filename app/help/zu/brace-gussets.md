Ipuleti lesixhasi lesiqinisi iphuleti eshiselwe ensikeni (noma ebhimini) isiqinisi esifika kuyo, endizeni yesiqinisi. I-DetailCraft ikhipha isiqinisi emgodini wonke wesixhumanisi, ibeka futhi ilinganise ipuleti lesixhasi, bese ixhumanisa isiqinisi nalo: **i-engle** noma **ibha esicaba** ngomugqa owodwa wamabhawodi, noma **ithubhu** (i-HSS esikwayo, enxande noma ejikelezayo) elifakwa phezu kwepuleti lesixhasi bese lishiselwa.

## Lapho isebenza khona

- Ukuphela kwesiqinisi kulele emgqeni wensika noma webhimu (ngaphakathi kwe-1 mm), futhi isiqinisi asiseduze kakhulu nalowo mugqa. Insika iyisekelo uma ikhona; uma kungenjalo, kuba yibhimu.
- Ipuleti lesixhasi lilele endizeni yesiqinisi nomugqa wesekelo. I-**engle** kumele ibe nohlangothi lwayo olulodwa kulowo ndiza, futhi i-**ibha esicaba** kumele ibe ngobuso bayo: phendula isiqinisi (ngokwe-{{ui:Rotation (°)}}) size ifike kulokho. Uma ama-engle ehlangothini le-45° endizeni, i-DetailCraft iyakushoyo futhi ayakhi lutho.
- Izinhlobo ze-I namachannel njengezinqinisi azixhunyiwe.

## Engeza amapuleti esixhasi

1. Khetha isiqinisi esisodwa noma ngaphezulu.
2. Khetha {{ui:Gusset}} eqenjini {{ui:Connect}} kumenyu {{ui:More}}.
3. Yonke imiphetho yezinqinisi ekhethiwe ehlala ensikeni noma ebhimini futhi ingenalo ipuleti lesixhasi ithola elilodwa.

Khetha ipuleti lesixhasi (chofoza iphuleti) ukuze ushintshe izilungiselelo zalo. {{ui:Apply to similar (n)}} ikopisha kuyo yonke imiphetho yesiqinisi engenalutho enephrofayili efanayo yesiqinisi nensika.

## Lokho i-DetailCraft ekuqondayo

- **Ukusikwa emuva.** Isiqinisi sifinyezwa kuleyo mphetho size amakhona aso acace, ngokuthi {{ui:Brace clearance}} (25), zonke izinto ezisesixhumanisweni (eceleni kwayo, noma ngaphesheya komphetho wayo uma into ipheleliswa lapho) nepuleti lesisekelo ngaphansi kwensika.
- **Amabhawodi (ama-engle, amabha).** {{ui:Bolts}} (3) ngomugqa owodwa ohlangothini lwesiqinisi, okokuqala {{ui:Bolt end distance}} (40) kusukela emphethelweni osikiwe, ngo-{{ui:Bolt pitch}} (75). Kuma-engle adlula ohlangothini lwesixhasi endizeni, {{ui:Bolt gauge}} kusukela esithendeni (0 = phakathi kohlangothi); kubha esicaba phakathi komugqa wayo ophakathi (noma ngaphandle kwawo ngegeji). Ukubamba kwebhawodi = uhlangothi noma ibha + ipuleti lesixhasi.
- **Ipuleti lesixhasi.** Irektangula: kusukela ebusweni besekelo iye ngaphesheya kwebhawodi lokugcina ngobude bomphetho wamabhawodi, futhi ihambe ngokuhambisana nesekelo njengoba amabhawodi edinga. Lihlala ngemuva kohlangothi lwe-engle (noma ebusweni bebha). Kuthubhu idlula phakathi kwethubhu, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Tube lap on the gusset}} (200) yethubhu, kukhona indawo yokushisela.
- **Izixhumanisi.** Esisekelweni sensika ipuleti lesixhasi lithwalwa liye phansi phezu kwepuleti lesisekelo. Lapho ibhimu liwela ngaphezu kwesixhumanisi (isiqinisi esiphakama kusukela ekhoneni lebhimu-insika), ipuleti lesixhasi lihlala ngaphansi noma ngaphezu kwebhimu. Uma lokhu kususa amabhawodi noma ukugoqwa esizindeni sawo somphetho, i-DetailCraft iyaxwayisa.
- **Ukushisela.** Ipuleti lesixhasi kusisekelo salo, zombili izinhlangothi. Ithubhu kupuleti lesixhasi, imigqa emine ngesigaba sokugoqwa.

## Okufanele wazi

- Ipuleti lesixhasi lingeleyo inhlanganisela yesisekelo (ishiselwe emsebenzini wokwakha). Isiqinisi sifika nemigodi yaso ibhoshwe, futhi siya kwifayela lalo le-NC.
- Isikhala sethubhu asisikwa emodelini; [ukuhlola ukungqubuzana](topic:clash-check) kushiya isiqinisi nepuleti lesixhasi lawo ngaphandle.
- Ipuleti lesixhasi alixhunyelwe nebhimu elithintayo, namapuleti esixhasi ahlanganiswe kubhimu nensika ngokushisela, namakhona aganqekile, nesakhiwo sokuhlelwa kwamaphoyinti womsebenzi we-Uniform Force Method akwenziwa okwamanje.
- I-DetailCraft ayiklami ipuleti lesixhasi noma amabhawodi (akukho ukuhlola amandla); izexwayiso zingokuhlukana nokulingana kuphela.
