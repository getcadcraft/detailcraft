In a Live TeamWork session, DetailCraft draws each person's pointer in the model with their name and colour, and puts a box around what they selected. The people list lets you follow one of them.

## What you'll do

Move your view with another person's, and, as the host, ask everyone to follow yours. Starting or joining the session is in CadCraft Studio; see [Detail one structure together with Live TeamWork](topic:work-live).

## Steps

1. In the session, choose {{ui:People}}. The {{ui:People in this session}} list shows each person's tool, sheet and last edit.
2. Choose {{ui:Follow}} beside a name. Your view moves with theirs, and the bar says you are following them.
3. Choose {{ui:Stop following}} (or {{ui:Stop}} in the bar) to go back to your own view.
4. As the host, choose {{ui:Ask everyone to follow me}}. Everyone is asked; choose {{ui:Stop asking everyone to follow me}} to end it.
5. Close the list with {{ui:Close}}.

## Notes

- If the person you follow is on a view that cannot be followed, you are told and your view stays.
- Following changes only what you see, not the model.
